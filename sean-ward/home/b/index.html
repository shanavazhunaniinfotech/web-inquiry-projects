    Questions

    Questions we hear every week.

    How much does a custom home cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?

    Most Ward homes land between $205 and $370 per square foot, depending on size, finishes and site work, not including land. Try the cost estimator for a quick range. You get an exact fixed price once your design is complete.

    Can you build on my lot?

    Yes. We build on your lot anywhere in our DFW service area. Before you commit, we’ll walk the lot with you for free and flag anything that affects cost, like slope, soil, trees or utilities.

    I don’t have land yet. Can you help?

    Yes. We know the communities across Collin, Denton, Tarrant, Rockwall and Dallas counties and can show you lots, or one of our available homes, that fit your budget.

    How long does it take to build?

    Design and permitting usually take 2–3 months, and construction takes 7–10 months for most homes. You get a written schedule with a date for every stage before we start.

    What’s included in the fixed price?

    Everything in your plans and selections: design, permits, site work, construction, finishes, landscaping and final cleaning. The price only changes if you ask for a change, and you approve every change in writing first.

    Do you help with financing?

    We work with local lenders who specialize in construction-to-permanent loans and can introduce you on the first call, so you know your numbers before you design.

    What warranty do you offer?

    Every home comes with a written 1-2-10 warranty: 1 year on workmanship and materials, 2 years on mechanical systems and 10 years on the structure.

    Still wondering about something? Talk to Sean directly. No sales team, no pressure.
