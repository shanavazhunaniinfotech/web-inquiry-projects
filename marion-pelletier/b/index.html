    Help

    Good to know.

    How long does delivery take?

    Orders placed before 2pm (Paris time) ship the same day. 1–2 working days in France, 2–4 days across the EU. Free over €80, otherwise €5.90.

    What is your returns policy?

    30 days to return any unused item for a full refund. Returns are free — print the prepaid label from your account.

    Why do handmade pieces vary slightly?

    Each piece is shaped, glazed or poured by hand, so small differences in colour and form are natural — they make your piece one of a kind.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna (pay in 3, interest-free). Every payment is encrypted and 3-D Secure.

    Do you ship outside the EU?

    Yes — to the UK, Switzerland, Norway and the US. Duties and delivery times are shown at checkout before you pay.

    Can I send an order as a gift?

    Switch on gift wrap in your bag, add a message, and we'll leave prices off the packing slip.
