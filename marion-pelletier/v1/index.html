    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Still unsure? Our Paris team answers within 2 hours, Monday to Saturday.

    Chat with us
    How long does delivery take?

    Orders placed before 2pm (Paris time) ship the same day. Delivery takes 1–2 working days in France and 2–4 days across the EU. Shipping is free over €80, otherwise €5.90.

    What is your returns policy?

    You have 30 days to return any unused item for a full refund. Returns are free — print the prepaid label from your account and drop it at any pick-up point.

    Why do handmade pieces vary slightly?

    Each piece is shaped, glazed or poured by hand, so small differences in colour and form are natural — they're what make your piece one of a kind.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna (pay in 3 interest-free instalments). Every payment is encrypted and 3-D Secure.

    Do you ship outside the EU?

    Yes — we ship to the UK, Switzerland, Norway and the US. Duties and delivery times are shown at checkout before you pay.

    Can I send an order as a gift?

    Of course. Tick “gift wrap” in your bag, add a message, and we'll leave prices off the packing slip.
