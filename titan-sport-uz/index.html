            Design notes · for Bilol

            Iron & Chalk

            The concept comes from the gym floor itself: raw cast iron (dark sections) and magnesium chalk (light sections). Category colours follow the international weight-plate code (red 25, blue 20, yellow 15, green 10, white 5), so the colour you see on the site can be the colour on shelf labels in the showroom.

            What is real

            • All 14 products, names, prices, old prices, weights, materials and specs come from your current site's product data.
            • Delivery (Tashkent 3 h, regions 24 h, free from 1,000,000 so'm), payment on receipt (cash, Click, Payme), phone, Telegram, address and hours come from your current site.

            What is left out on purpose

            • Star ratings, review counts, "10,000+ athletes" and "50+ tonnes" — these could not be verified, so they are not shown. Add real reviews to the reserved slot.
            • The public "Admin" link — it should not be on a customer-facing site.

            Placeholders

            • Product images are technical drawings. They are meant to be replaced with your own studio photos (plain background, side view, same angle for every product).
            • Old (crossed-out) prices are shown as on your current site. Please confirm they are real former prices.
            • Checkout and the consultation form are demos: nothing is sent. The site check (orders not reaching you) still needs a back end or WooCommerce.
