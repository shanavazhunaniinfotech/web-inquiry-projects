Questions, answered

Still wondering? Email our team. A real person replies.

What makes FaithFits different from other scrub brands?

Premium performance fabric meets purpose. Every set is made with a four-way stretch, anti-microbial blend and carries a scripture detail on the sleeve, designed for healthcare workers who see their work as a calling.

Are FaithFits scrubs comfortable for long shifts?

Yes. Our 72% polyester, 21% rayon, 7% spandex twill is breathable, medium-weight and stretches in every direction, built for 12-hour shifts and beyond.

Do you offer men's and women's scrubs?

Yes. Both come in jogger sets in 11 colors, and our new Wide Leg set gives women a relaxed silhouette. All sets come in XS–XL.

How should I care for my scrubs?

Machine wash cold with like colors, tumble dry low and skip the bleach. Washing inside out keeps the embroidery looking new.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes, free shipping on every US domestic order, with no minimum.

What if my size doesn't fit?

We'll help you exchange it for the right size. Reach out to our team and we'll make it right.
