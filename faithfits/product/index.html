Can I order a different size for the top and pants?

Yes. Choose your top size and pants size separately, for example a Medium top with Small pants. They ship together as one set.

That's what they're built for: breathable, medium-weight twill with four-way stretch and an anti-microbial finish.

Yes. Wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low to keep the scripture and FF badge looking new.
