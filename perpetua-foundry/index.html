    Security & trust

    Your people's data, handled with care

    Perpetua LIS is designed so each customer's organisation, learners and records stay separate and protected — from the first payment to every login.

    Ask our team a security question

    • Protected payments

      Card details are handled by our payment provider and never stored on our servers.

    • Verified access

      Workspaces are activated only after payment is verified — no duplicate accounts or charges.

    • Organisation isolation

      Each customer's data is separated, with role-based admin permissions.

    • Privacy by design

      HTTPS everywhere, clear consent controls and data used only for agreed purposes.

    Trust statements are draft copy; certification references will only be published once approved by Perpetua Foundry.
