Our story

We exist because sitting shouldn't hurt.

Back pain is the number one cause of workplace absence worldwide — yet most products designed to address it had barely evolved in decades. Ordinary cushions offered padding. Basic lumbar supports offered rigidity.

Our team of ergonomics researchers and health-tech engineers drew on graphene material science and orthopedic research to build something different: a seat that supports natural posture and makes long sitting hours comfortable.

01What makes ErgoGraphene different from a regular seat cushion?

Unlike ordinary cushions that only add padding, ErgoGraphene combines graphene-enhanced comfort technology with orthopedic-inspired lumbar alignment. The multi-zone design supports your back, waist and hips simultaneously, and the breathable tech-mesh prevents heat build-up.

02Can I use it in my car as well as my office chair?

Absolutely. It fits office chairs, car seats, gaming chairs and home sofas. Its compact, portable form means you can move it between your desk and your car without any hassle.

03How long does it take to feel a difference?

Most customers notice improved support from the very first use. By week 1 your lower back feels more supported, by week 2 long sits feel more comfortable, and by week 4 it becomes part of your routine.

04How long will delivery take, and is shipping really free?

Yes — shipping is free worldwide with no minimum spend. From dispatch: USA & Canada 4–10 business days, Europe & UK 5–12, Australia & NZ 5–12, Asia 3–7, rest of world 7–14. You'll get a tracking link by email.

05What is your return policy?

30 days. Contact support@ergographene.com, return it unused in the original packaging with our prepaid label, and we refund your original payment method within 10 business days of receiving it. Sale items and gift cards are non-refundable.

06What if my order arrives damaged or gets lost?

Every shipment is insured. Photograph any damage and contact us within 48 hours for a replacement or full refund. If a parcel is lost, we file the claim and reship or refund in full.

07How do I clean my cushion?

Wipe the tech-mesh with a soft damp cloth or mild fabric cleaner. For a deeper clean, hand-wash the cover in cool water. Avoid machine washing and air-dry away from direct sunlight.

08Will it lose its shape over time?

It's built with high-resilience ergonomic materials that keep their shape with daily use. Store it flat or upright, don't compress it for long periods and keep it out of prolonged direct sunlight.

09Does ErgoGraphene come with a warranty?

Yes — the 30-Day Comfort Guarantee covers manufacturing defects such as stitching failure, structural deformation or a fault with the graphene layer. We replace or refund at no cost.

10What's not covered?

Normal wear and tear, damage from machine washing or tumble drying, accidental cuts or spills, exposure to extreme heat or sunlight, and minor colour fading after extended use.
