Will it fit my chair or car seat?

Yes. It fits office chairs, car seats, gaming chairs and home sofas. It's compact and light, so you can move it from your desk to your car in seconds.

How is it different from a regular cushion?

Regular cushions only add padding. ErgoGraphene has a contoured, multi-zone shape that supports your back, waist and hips together, plus a graphene warming layer and breathable tech-mesh.

How soon will I feel a difference?

Most customers notice better support from the first use. By week 1 your lower back feels more supported, by week 2 long sits feel easier, and by week 4 it's part of your routine.

How long does delivery take?

Shipping is free worldwide. Europe & UK: 5–12 business days from dispatch. USA & Canada: 4–10. Every parcel is insured and tracked.

What if it's not for me?

You have 30 days. Return it unused in its original packaging with our prepaid label, and we refund you within 10 business days of receiving it.

How do I clean it?

Wipe the mesh with a soft damp cloth. For a deeper clean, hand-wash the cover in cool water and air-dry. Please don't machine wash or tumble dry.
