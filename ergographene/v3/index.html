Meet ErgoGraphene

A seat cushion focused on your spine.

Not another flat foam pad. Designed with ergonomics researchers and health-tech engineers, ErgoGraphene is built on a simple principle: support the natural curve of the lower back, so the rest of your body can relax.

The butterfly back meets your mid-spine, two kidney wings wrap your lower back and six reinforced ribs hold the lumbar curve. There is no setup and no levers — strap it to the chair you already own, and sit.
