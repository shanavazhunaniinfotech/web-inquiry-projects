Is Pure Nur really halal?

Yes. Both formulas use collagen from halal-slaughtered cattle and are certified by an accredited halal authority. We publish the full certificate on our Halal page so you can check it yourself.

What is the collagen made from?

Hydrolysed bovine collagen peptides (Type I & III). It is never porcine (pork) and never from an unspecified source.

Does it taste of anything?

No. Both formulas are unflavoured and dissolve fully in hot or cold drinks, so you can add them to coffee, tea, smoothies or water without changing the taste.

How should I take it, and when will I notice a difference?

Take one 10 g scoop a day, at any time. Collagen works best as a daily habit, so we suggest giving it at least 8–12 weeks of consistent use.

Which one is right for me?

Choose Collagen if you want pure collagen and nothing else. Choose Glow if you'd like added vitamin C, zinc, biotin and hyaluronic acid for your beauty routine. Can't decide? The Duo saves you 15%.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, any time, in one click from your account. There's no minimum term and no fees. You can also skip, pause or swap formulas.

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 1pm are dispatched the same working day from Glasgow. UK delivery usually takes 1–3 working days and is free over £40 and on all subscriptions.

Can I take it while pregnant or breastfeeding?

If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or under medical supervision, please speak to your doctor before taking any food supplement.
