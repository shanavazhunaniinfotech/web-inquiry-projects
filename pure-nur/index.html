Is Pure Nur really halal?

Yes. Both formulas use collagen from halal-slaughtered cattle and are certified by an accredited halal authority. We publish the full certificate so you can check it yourself.

What is the collagen made from?

Hydrolysed bovine collagen peptides (Type I & III). Never porcine (pork) and never from an unspecified source.

Does it taste of anything?

No. Both formulas are unflavoured and dissolve fully in hot or cold drinks, so coffee still tastes like coffee.

When will I notice a difference?

Collagen works best as a daily habit. We suggest one scoop a day for at least 8–12 weeks.

Collagen or Glow: which is right for me?

Choose Collagen for pure collagen and nothing else. Choose Glow if you'd like vitamin C, zinc, biotin and hyaluronic acid for your beauty routine. Can't decide? The Duo saves 15%.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, any time, in one click from your account. No minimum term and no fees. You can also skip, pause or swap formulas.

How fast is delivery?

Order before 1pm for same-day dispatch from Glasgow. UK delivery usually takes 1–3 working days and is free over £40 and on every subscription.
