Your first session

Starting therapy takes courage. Here's what to expect.

You don't need to have everything figured out before you reach out.

  1. You'll get a confirmation and a short online intake form, so you don't have to repeat your story on the day.

  2. Level 7, St James Trust Building, opposite Hyde Park. Look for the door labelled "The Psychology Alley". Or join by telehealth from anywhere private.

  3. An initial assessment. We talk about what's brought you here and what you'd like to change. You set the pace.

  4. You leave with a shared understanding and a plan that fits your life. You decide whether to continue. There's no lock-in.

Getting a Mental Health Care Plan

  1. Book a longer appointment with your GP and say it's for a Mental Health Care Plan.
  2. Your GP will ask a few questions and can address the referral to The Psychology Alley.
  3. Send us the referral by email. We'll add it to your file before your session.
  4. Claim up to 10 rebated sessions each calendar year. Your GP reviews after the first 6.

A plan is not required to start therapy.
