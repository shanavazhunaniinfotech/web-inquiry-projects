02 · Solo to group practice

Where the site still says "I" while the practice says "we".

Your brief asks for a refresh that reflects the move to a group practice. These are the exact places that still describe a solo practice.

Booking form offers one psychologist

"Choose a Psychologist" lists only No preference and Dr. Heather Gan (Mon, Thu, & Fri). As clinicians join, this is the first place clients look for them.

/make-an-appointment

Written in the first person

"I am a psychologist in Sydney CBD, and I use…", "I do not do couples' therapy". Fine for a solo practice, confusing for a team.

/psychologist-sydney-cbd · /psychologist-about · /faqs-for-psychologists

"Meet Our Psychologist", singular

The therapy pages end with a single-clinician block, so clients reading about DBT or Schema Therapy only ever see one person.

/cbt · /dbt · /schema-therapy · /theraphies

The FAQ contradicts a service page

The FAQ says "…or who are involved in court cases as I am not trained to provide medico-legal or insurance reports", while a full page offers Single Expert Witness reports for the Family Court.

/faqs-for-psychologists vs /single-expert-reports-family-court

Family report enquiries go to another address

The FAQ directs Family Report enquiries to reception@therelationspace.com.au. If that's intended, great; if not, those enquiries are leaving your practice.

/faqs-for-psychologists

"Therapies" opens the CBT page

The /theraphies address shows the CBT page word for word (it points search engines to /cbt, so SEO is safe). Visitors expecting an overview of all 8 therapies never get one.

/theraphies
