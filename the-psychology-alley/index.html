FAQs

Questions people ask before they reach out.

Can't see yours? Call (02) 9085 5864 or email info@thepsychologyalley.com.

Do I need a referral or a diagnosis?

No. You can start without either. A Mental Health Care Plan from your GP is only needed if you'd like to claim the Medicare rebate, and you can get one after you start.

How soon can I be seen?

We aim to call you within one business day of your enquiry and offer a first appointment within [X] weeks. Registrars usually have the shortest wait.

How do I know which psychologist is right for me?

Tell us what's going on in the short questionnaire. We'll suggest a psychologist based on their experience, your availability and your budget, and confirm it with you on the intake call.

What's the difference between a clinical psychologist and a registrar?

Clinical psychologists have completed advanced training in assessing and treating complex mental health difficulties. Registrars are fully registered psychologists completing that training under supervision, at a lower fee and Medicare rebate.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 48 hours' notice. Cancellations within 24–48 hours are charged at 50%, and within 24 hours at the full fee. [confirm: current site wording conflicts]

What don't you offer?

We work with adults (18+) and don't currently provide couples therapy, eating-disorder treatment, or therapy for people involved in active court proceedings. We're happy to suggest other services.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Your information is kept private, except in rare situations such as serious risk of harm or a court subpoena. We'll explain these in your first session.

Getting a Mental Health Care Plan

  1. Book a longer appointment (20+ min) with your GP and say it's for a Mental Health Care Plan.
  2. Your GP will ask a few questions and may give you a referral to The Psychology Alley.
  3. Send us the referral (email or upload). We'll add it to your file before your session.
  4. Claim up to 10 rebated sessions each calendar year. Your GP reviews after 6.

You can start therapy before you have a plan. Rebates apply from the date of the referral.
