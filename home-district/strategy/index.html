Home page concept · design rationale

A magazine that sells, calmly.

Version 2 · "Issue 01". The home page has one job: turn a first-time visitor into a buyer or a qualified lead, while feeling like an interior design magazine. Every section below earns its place by removing a reason not to buy.

Tip: on the home page, tap “Why this layout?” (bottom-left) to see these notes on each section.

Built on your brand guide

White leads

White canvas, Cloud (#F7F7F7) to separate sections, black for actions only. Roughly the 65 / 20 / 10 / 5 balance from the guide.

The furniture supplies the warmth

Brand graphics stay monochrome. Warmth comes from daylight photography, wood and textiles, exactly as the guide describes.

Inter + an editorial serif

Inter for all copy, UI and buttons, as the guide proposes. The guide notes "type choices can develop with the store", so headlines add Instrument Serif italic accents for the interior-magazine feel. The logo itself is untouched.

One clear action

Black buttons with white text; secondary actions in black outline. 4 px corners, 8 px spacing, 44 px tap targets.

Logo untouched

The supplied wordmark artwork, at 180 px on desktop, always on white with clear space. No font substitute.

Honest, useful copy

No "ultimate luxury". We name real details: kiln-dried frames, zip-off covers, delivery times, payment splits.

The section order — and why

The page follows the questions a furniture buyer asks, in the order they ask them: Is this for me? Can I trust it? What do you have? Is it good? Why so affordable? Will it fit? Who are you?

  1. Hero: a magazine cover that opens into a room

    "The designer look, at a fair price." in large editorial type, with a small arched window in the headline. On scroll the arch opens into a full-screen room with a shoppable sofa. Shop and Book a free home visit, plus rating and tabby/tamara, are visible before the first scroll.

    ClarityMemorability

  2. Promise marquee: remove risk

    Free delivery and assembly, pay in 4, 5-year guarantee, 30-day trial and free home visit, always in motion and always in view.

    Risk reversal

  3. Rooms: a horizontal gallery

    On desktop the page turns sideways like magazine pages; on mobile it is a swipe rail. Every room links straight to its products.

    Navigation

  4. The edit: bestsellers

    A large featured product with colour switching and Add to cart, then a grid where each card shows the 4-payment price, a room view on hover, a one-tap "+" add, wishlist and Quick view.

    Social proofBNPL framing

  5. Manifesto: why it costs less

    A statement that lights up word by word as you scroll, then a price comparison, a "how we price" breakdown and four hard numbers. It turns a low price into a reason to trust.

    Objection handling

  6. Shop the room

    A cinematic full-width room with tap-to-shop dots and a panel that totals the room with a 5% bundle saving and the 4-payment price.

    AOV

  7. Anatomy of comfort

    As you scroll, the image zooms into the frame, foam, covers and fabric, each with its real-life benefit. Ends with free fabric swatches.

    Quality proofMicro-conversion

  8. Tailor-made configurator

    A live drawing of the sofa that changes with design, width, fabric and legs, with an instant price. Add to cart (made to order) or talk to a stylist with the design attached.

    EngagementLead quality

  9. Reviews as pull-quotes

    Large serif quotes with photos of customers' rooms, each answering an objection and linking to the product.

    Social proof

  10. Free home visit: 3 easy steps

    Tap a room, pick a date from the next 7 days, then leave a name and mobile. WhatsApp is always the alternative.

    Lead capture

  11. The people behind it

    Founder quote, faces, a hand-drawn signature and a real studio address.

    Brand trust

  12. Community, FAQ, launch list

    Drifting rows of customer homes; objections answered with WhatsApp beside them; and a January 2027 early-access list with AED 250 off.

    Pre-launch

Always-on conversion features

WhatsApp buttonAlways visible; the way most UAE customers prefer to ask questions. Styled in brand black with the recognisable icon.
tabby / tamara everywhereOn cards, quick view, cart and checkout, so big prices feel manageable at every step.
Cart drawerAdds without leaving the page. Shows free delivery, the 4-payment price and a "Complete the room" suggestion.
Quick viewA preview of the product page: colours, sizes, delivery date, guarantee and free swatches.
SearchInstant results with popular searches.
Mobile firstThumb-friendly 44 px targets, swipeable rails, full-width CTAs, no horizontal scrolling.
Calm motionSmooth scrolling, slow reveals and parallax (GSAP + Lenis), a quiet custom cursor on desktop. All switched off for people who prefer reduced motion.
Mobile action barAfter the hero, a slim bar keeps Shop, Free home visit and WhatsApp one thumb-tap away.
