Home page concept · design rationale

A showroom that sells, calmly.

The home page has one job: turn a first-time visitor into a buyer or a qualified lead, while feeling like an interior design magazine. Every section below earns its place by removing a reason not to buy.

Tip: on the home page, tap “Why this layout?” (bottom-left) to see these notes on each section.

Built on your brand guide

White leads

White canvas, Cloud (#F7F7F7) to separate sections, black for actions only. Roughly the 65 / 20 / 10 / 5 balance from the guide.

The furniture supplies the warmth

Brand graphics stay monochrome. Warmth comes from daylight photography, wood and textiles, exactly as the guide describes.

Inter, sentence case

Inter throughout: Regular copy, Medium headings, Semibold buttons. Large, tightly spaced headlines give the editorial, magazine feel.

One clear action

Black buttons with white text; secondary actions in black outline. 4 px corners, 8 px spacing, 44 px tap targets.

Logo untouched

The supplied wordmark artwork, at 180 px on desktop, always on white with clear space. No font substitute.

Honest, useful copy

No "ultimate luxury". We name real details: kiln-dried frames, zip-off covers, delivery times, payment splits.

The section order — and why

The page follows the questions a furniture buyer asks, in the order they ask them: Is this for me? Can I trust it? What do you have? Is it good? Why so affordable? Will it fit? Who are you?

  1. Hero — the promise in one line

    "The designer look, at a fair price." The positioning is understood in 3 seconds. Two paths: Shop (ready buyers) and Book a free home visit (considered buyers). A shoppable tag on the photo and a trust row (rating, free delivery, 0% instalments) sit above the fold.

    ClarityTrust

  2. Promise bar — remove risk

    Free delivery and assembly, pay in 4 with tabby/tamara, 30-day returns, 5-year frame guarantee. These answer the biggest fears of buying furniture online before a product is shown.

    Risk reversal

  3. Shop by room — fast self-sorting

    People shop for a room, not a SKU. Large photographic tiles (swipeable on mobile) get them to the right range in one tap.

    Navigation

  4. Most loved — social proof that sells

    Bestsellers with filters. Every card shows price and the 4-payment price, colour swatches that switch the photo, ratings, wishlist, Quick view and Add to cart, so buying never needs more than two taps.

    Bandwagon effectBNPL framing

  5. Why it costs less — justify the price

    "Affordable luxury" raises a doubt: is cheaper worse? A clear comparison shows where showroom money goes and why ours goes into the product. This turns low price into a reason to trust, not a warning sign.

    Objection handling

  6. Shop the room — raise order value

    A styled room with tap-to-shop dots and "Add the whole room · save 5%". Furniture is bought as a look; bundling lifts average order value.

    AOV

  7. Considered details + free swatches

    Proof of the quality people can't touch online: frame, foam, covers, fabric. Free swatches give hesitant buyers a small, free first step.

    Quality proofMicro-conversion

  8. Tailor-made — an instant price

    A mini builder (design, width, fabric) with a live estimate instead of a vague "contact us". The chosen design flows into the home-visit form, so leads arrive qualified.

    EngagementLead quality

  9. Reviews — real homes

    Customer photos, verified badges and a rating summary. Each review answers an objection: quality, fit, practicality.

    Social proof

  10. Free home visit — the high-ticket closer

    Many buyers won't purchase a sofa on first visit. A free, no-obligation visit captures them as leads. Short form, +971 mobile, WhatsApp alternative.

    Lead capture

  11. Our story — the people behind the brand

    Founder note, faces and a studio address. A new brand needs to feel accountable and human.

    Brand trust

  12. Community — shoppable inspiration

    Customer photos from Instagram, each linking to products.

  13. FAQ — stop exits

    Delivery, assembly, tabby/tamara, returns and guarantee answered on the page, with WhatsApp for anything else.

    Objection handling

  14. Launch list — sell before January

    Early access and AED 250 off builds a launch-day audience. After launch it becomes the newsletter sign-up.

    Pre-launch
