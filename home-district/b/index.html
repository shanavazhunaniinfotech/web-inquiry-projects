    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Ask a real person
    How long does delivery take?

    In-stock pieces arrive in 3–10 days anywhere in the UAE. Tailor-made pieces take 6–8 weeks. We confirm a 2-hour window by WhatsApp the day before.

    Is assembly really free?

    Yes. Our own team carries everything to the room you choose, assembles it and takes all the packaging away, in every Emirate.

    How do tabby and tamara work?

    Choose tabby or tamara at checkout and split your order into 4 interest-free payments. No fees when you pay on time.

    What if it doesn’t suit my room?

    Live with in-stock furniture for 30 days. If it’s not right, we collect it and refund you within 7 days.

    What does the guarantee cover?

    Every frame, spring and joint for 5 years. If anything fails under normal use, we repair or replace it free.

    Can I see the furniture in person?

    Visit our Al Quoz studio by appointment, or book a free home visit and we’ll bring samples to you.
