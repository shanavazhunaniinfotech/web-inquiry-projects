    11Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Can’t find what you need? A real person replies on WhatsApp, 9am–9pm, seven days a week.

    Chat with us
    How long does delivery take?

    In-stock pieces arrive in 3–10 days anywhere in the UAE. Tailor-made pieces take 6–8 weeks. We confirm a 2-hour delivery window by WhatsApp the day before.

    Is assembly really free?

    Yes. Our own team delivers to your room of choice, assembles everything and takes all the packaging away — at no extra cost, in every Emirate.

    How do tabby and tamara work?

    Choose tabby or tamara at checkout and split your order into 4 interest-free payments. The first is taken at checkout, the rest monthly. No fees when you pay on time.

    What if it doesn’t suit my room?

    You have 30 days to return in-stock furniture in its original condition. We collect it from your home and refund you within 7 days. Tailor-made pieces can’t be returned, which is why we offer free swatches and home visits first.

    What does the 5-year guarantee cover?

    Every frame, spring and joint, for 5 years from delivery. If anything fails under normal use, we repair or replace it free.

    Can I see the furniture in person?

    Yes — visit our studio in Al Quoz by appointment, or book a free home visit and we’ll bring the samples to you.
