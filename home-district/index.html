    How long does delivery take?

    In-stock pieces arrive in 3–10 days anywhere in the UAE. Tailor-made pieces take 6–8 weeks. We confirm a 2-hour window by WhatsApp the day before.

    Is assembly really free?

    Yes. Our own team carries everything to the room you choose, assembles it and takes all the packaging away — in every Emirate, at no cost.

    How do tabby and tamara work?

    Choose tabby or tamara at checkout and split your order into 4 interest-free payments. The first is taken at checkout, the rest monthly. No fees when you pay on time.

    What if it doesn’t suit my room?

    Live with in-stock furniture for 30 days. If it’s not right, we collect it and refund you within 7 days. Tailor-made pieces can’t be returned — which is why swatches and home visits are free.

    What does the 5-year guarantee cover?

    Every frame, spring and joint for 5 years from delivery. If anything fails under normal use, we repair or replace it free.

    Can I see the furniture in person?

    Yes — visit our Al Quoz studio by appointment, or book a free home visit and we’ll bring samples to you.
