Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Write to stay@haldenhouse.com. A real person replies the same day.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. When you book with us, you can move your dates for free up to seven days before you arrive. After that we'll always do our best to help.

Are children and dogs welcome?

Children of every age are welcome, and the Cottage is made for families. Well-behaved dogs stay free in our Barn Rooms, with a bed and bowl waiting.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 11am. If your room is free, we're happy to let you arrive early or stay late.

Do I need to stay to eat in the restaurant?

No. Dinner and weekend lunch are open to everyone. There are only nine tables, so we suggest booking ahead.

Is there Wi-Fi and phone signal?

Yes, fast Wi-Fi in every room and good signal in the house. We just don't have televisions, by choice.
