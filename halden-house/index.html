Our way

We do fewer things, and we do them properly. A bed that is made well. Bread made that morning. A room with nothing in it that does not need to be there.

  1. 01

    Made slowly

    Linen washed in the sun, floors oiled by hand, a menu that changes when the weather does.

  2. 02

    Kept small

    Twelve rooms, so we know your name by the second morning and the house never feels busy.

  3. 03

    Left quiet

    No televisions, no piped music. Books, a fire, and the fields outside every window.

Good to know

Questions people often ask.

Anything else? Write to stay@haldenhouse.com and a person will answer the same day.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. When you book here, you can change your dates for free up to seven days before you arrive. After that, we will always try to help.

Are children and dogs welcome?

Children of all ages are welcome, and the Cottage is made for families. Well-behaved dogs can stay in our Barn Rooms, with a bed and bowl waiting.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 11am. If your room is free, we are happy to let you arrive early or leave late.

Do I need to be staying to eat here?

No. Dinner and weekend lunch are open to everyone. We do suggest booking a table, as there are only a few.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, fast Wi-Fi in every room. We just do not have televisions, by choice.
