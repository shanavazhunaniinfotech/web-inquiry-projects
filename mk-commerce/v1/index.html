FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days and usually arrive within 7–12 business days. Every order includes a tracking number, and shipping is free on orders over $35.

What if my pet doesn't like it?

You're covered by our 30-day Happy Pet Promise. Reach out within 30 days of delivery and we'll arrange a refund or replacement.

Is the AirTag included with the collar?

No. The collar has a snug, waterproof holder designed for Apple AirTag, sold separately. The reflective strip and bell are included.

Will the brush scratch my pet's skin?

No. The pins have rounded safety tips that massage while they collect loose hair. Always brush gently in the direction of hair growth.

Does it work for cats and long-haired breeds?

Yes. The brush and glove work on cats and dogs of all sizes with short, medium, curly or long coats.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal, all through Shopify's secure, encrypted checkout.
