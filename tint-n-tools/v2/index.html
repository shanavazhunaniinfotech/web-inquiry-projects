How long does it take?

Most cars take 2–4 hours. Relax in our lounge or drop it off. Homes and offices vary by size, and you'll get an exact time with your quote.

Is ceramic worth the upgrade?

If you drive or park in the sun, yes. It rejects much more heat than standard film, never fades and won't interfere with phone or GPS signals.

Is it legal?

Every state is different. We'll recommend a compliant shade and provide a certificate on request.

What if I spot a bubble later?

Bring it back. Our free re-do promise covers it for as long as you own the car.

When can I roll the windows down?

Wait 3–5 days while the film fully cures.
