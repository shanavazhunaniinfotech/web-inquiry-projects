How long does it take?

Most cars take 2–4 hours. Wait in our lounge or drop it off. Homes and offices depend on size, and you'll get an exact time with your quote.

Is ceramic really worth the extra?

If you park outside or drive in the heat, yes. Ceramic blocks far more heat than standard film, never fades and won't interfere with your phone or GPS.

Is it legal?

Laws vary by state. We'll recommend a legal shade for your vehicle and give you a compliance certificate.

What if I see a bubble later?

Bring it back. Our flawless finish promise means we fix it free, for as long as you own the car.

When can I roll my windows down?

Give it 3–5 days for the film to cure fully.
