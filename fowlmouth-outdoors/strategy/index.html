Redesign concepts · Home page + product page

Two ways to turn visitors into customers.

Both concepts use FowlMouth’s real products, photos and prices pulled from the current store. Every section is placed for a reason — tap “Why this layout?” on any page to see the reasoning right on top of the design.

The two directions

Warm, premium, editorial — like a field journal. Bone and marsh green with blaze-orange buttons (hunter orange is the one color the eye can’t miss). Feels like a brand worth $34 a hat and appeals to gift buyers as much as hunters.

What we found on the current store

Quick wins hiding in plain sight

No reviews or social proof anywhereThe biggest trust gap for a newer brand. Both concepts add reviews, ratings and customer photos.
Every product says “Sale price … Regular price”There’s no actual sale, which looks like a template glitch. We show one clean price.
Navigation overlaps“Apparel” and “Button-Up Shirts” go to the same page; “Wholesale” goes to the general contact page.
47 products, one long listOnly price/availability filters. We organize by pursuit: Waterfowl, Upland, Saltwater, Texas & Humor.
Hat of the Month is buriedYour recurring-revenue product sits in the menu. We give it a full section with both plans and the $30 saving.
Placeholders still liveFooter social links point to Shopify’s own accounts, and the refund policy still says “[INSERT RETURN ADDRESS]”.
Shipping policy is one sentenceNo rates, threshold or timing — a top reason people abandon a cart. We show dates and a free-shipping goal.
Some photos are in HEIC formatiPhone’s format; some browsers won’t show them. We’d convert them and compress images for speed.

Product page

Everything needed to say “yes” — above the fold

  • Swipeable gallery on mobile; thumbnails (A) or a large stacked grid (B) on desktop. Picking a color shows that exact hat.
  • Rating under the title, price with Shop Pay “4 payments”, photo swatches with sold-out options crossed out.
  • One bright Add to Cart button, Shop Pay express checkout, and a sticky add-to-cart bar on mobile.
  • Real arrival dates with today’s cut-off, plus 15-day returns and the “damaged? we fix it” promise.
  • Order-size boosters: decal add-on checkbox (B), “Frequently bought together” bundle, “pairs well with” hats.
  • Reviews with fit meter and photos, “You may also like”, and “Recently viewed”.

Try it: add a few hats — the cart drawer shows a live free-shipping progress bar and a smart add-on. Every product in the catalog works: Pearl Snap, Dove SZN, Camo Hoodie.
