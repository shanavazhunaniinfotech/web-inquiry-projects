Quick answers

Where does FinePS ship?

FinePS serves all 50 U.S. states and Washington, DC. Contiguous-state orders use checkout shipping options. Alaska and Hawaii orders require a shipping quote through the Contact page.

What shipping speeds are available?

Eligible orders can use Free Regular Shipping in 8–10 days, Standard Shipping in 4–7 days, or Expedited Shipping in 2–4 days. Paid rates are calculated at checkout from the destination, shipping profile and combined order weight.

How do I return a product?

Contact FinePS within 30 days of delivery and we'll provide the return label. After the returned product is received, FinePS issues an 80% merchandise refund. Original shipping is not refundable and exchanges are not offered.

How do I know which spline size I need?

Check the size printed on your old spline's packaging, or measure the old spline's diameter with calipers. If you're unsure, use the Product Finder or send us a photo of your frame channel and we'll match it.
