    How do I get the circuit after I buy it?

    Instantly. You get an email with your download link straight after checkout, and you can re-download it any time from your account.

    I'm a beginner. Can I do these?

    Yes. Every circuit comes in Foundation, Standard and Certified editions. Start on Foundation, scale movements with bands, and move up when you're ready.

    What kit do I need?

    Outdoor circuits need a pull-up bar and space to run. Gym circuits use a barbell or dumbbells. Bands, a weighted vest or a dip belt let you scale either way.

    Does the 10% member discount apply to gear?

    Yes — 10% off everything in the shop, applied automatically at checkout, including Saturday session bookings.

    Can I cancel the Members Club?

    Any time, from your account. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

    Do sessions run in bad weather?

    We run in all weather unless it's unsafe. If we cancel, you'll be moved to the next date or refunded. Can't make it? Email us 24 hours before and we'll move you.
