02 · Account request and approval

From request to wholesale access, with you in control.

  1. 1Request formA business fills in the wholesale form on your Wholesale page (Shopify Forms, styled to your Figma).
  2. 2Company created, no access yetShopify creates the company, location and contact. Ordering stays off until you approve.
  3. 3You approveOne click in Shopify admin. You can review the details first, or decline.
  4. 4Approval emailThe buyer gets a branded email with a link to log in.
  5. 5Wholesale catalogOnce logged in, they see wholesale prices, the minimum of 5 per SKU and your wholesale shipping.
