    How long does delivery take?

    Orders placed before 2pm EST are dispatched the same day. US delivery takes 2–4 business days and is complimentary on orders over $150. Next-day express is available at checkout.

    What is your returns policy?

    Enjoy free returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery. Items should be unworn with tags attached. Start a return online in under a minute — no printer required.

    How do I find my perfect size?

    Use our 10-second Fit Finder or view the detailed size guide. Every product page also lists model measurements and the size she's wearing.

    Do you ship internationally?

    Yes — we ship to 40+ countries with duties and taxes calculated at checkout, so there are never surprise fees on delivery.

    Can I pay in instalments?

    Absolutely. Select Shop Pay Installments at checkout to split your purchase into 4 interest-free payments.
