    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Still need help? Chat with us — we reply within a few hours.

    How long does shipping take?

    Orders placed before 2pm (EST) ship the same business day. Standard US delivery takes 3–5 business days and is free on orders over $100. Express (1–2 days) is available at checkout.

    What is your returns policy?

    We offer free returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn with tags attached. Start a return in seconds from our Returns page — no printer needed.

    How do I find my size?

    Use our quick Size Finder above or open the full size guide. Every product page also shows model measurements and the size they're wearing.

    Do you ship internationally?

    Yes — we ship to 40+ countries with duties and taxes calculated at checkout, so there are no surprise fees on delivery.

    Can I pay in instalments?

    Absolutely. Choose Shop Pay Installments at checkout to split your order into 4 interest-free payments.
