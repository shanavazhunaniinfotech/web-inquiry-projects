    2 · Membership or course?

    You don't have to choose. Combine them.

    Course only

    • Simple to sell, one-off payment
    • Build once, sell many times
    • Income stops after each sale
    • Lower completion without support

    Membership only

    • Recurring monthly income
    • Ongoing relationship and results
    • Needs fresh content every month
    • Members leave if value feels thin
    Recommended

    Course inside a membership, plus 1:1

    • Signature course included in the membership
    • Monthly live call keeps members engaged
    • 1:1 coaching as the premium tier
    • Three price points for three types of client
