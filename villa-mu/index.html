The villa

A haven of beauty & tranquility

Set in the heart of the Valle d'Itria, Villa Mu is a place to slow right down. Long breakfasts under the arches, afternoons by the pool, and evenings with a wood-fired supper as the sun drops behind the olive trees.

Inside, there are two king-size bedrooms, a twin, a small double, two shower rooms, a living room and a study. Outside, there's a fully equipped outdoor kitchen with a wood-burning oven, three separate dining areas and gardens with views across the Puglian countryside.

How many people does Villa Mu sleep?

Up to 8 guests across four bedrooms: two king-size bedrooms, a twin bedroom and a small double, with two shower rooms.

Which airports are closest?

Brindisi airport is about 45 minutes away and Bari airport just over an hour. Both have regular flights from the UK.

Do we need a car?

We'd recommend one. The villa is in peaceful countryside, and there's private parking for up to four cars within the grounds, behind electric gates.

Is the pool private?

Yes, it's for Villa Mu guests only. It's 1.5 metres deep, with six sun loungers, pool towels and an outdoor shower, and is maintained weekly.

Is there air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes. There are heating and air-conditioning units in the bedrooms and living areas, extra fans in each bedroom, and free Wi-Fi throughout.

Where can we eat out and shop nearby?

The nearest restaurant is 1 km away, and Cisternino has plenty of restaurants, shops and supermarkets. Our favourites include The Almond, Melting Pot Bistro, Il Cucco and Bar Pineta in Cisternino, and Torre del Angelico and Curcuma Pizzeria in Martina Franca.
