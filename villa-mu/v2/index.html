( FAQ )

Good to know

Still wondering? Message the owners on WhatsApp or call +44 7714 845140.

How many people does Villa Mu sleep?

Up to 8 guests in four bedrooms: two king-size, a twin and a small double, with two shower rooms.

Which airports are closest?

Brindisi is about 45 minutes away and Bari just over an hour, both with regular flights from the UK.

Do we need a car?

We'd recommend one. The villa is in quiet countryside, with private parking for up to four cars behind electric gates.

Is the pool private?

Yes, it's for Villa Mu guests only. It's 1.5 m deep, with six sun loungers, pool towels and an outdoor shower, and is maintained weekly.

Is there air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes. There's heating and A/C in the bedrooms and living areas, fans in each bedroom, and free Wi-Fi.

Where can we eat out nearby?

The closest restaurant is 1 km away. In Cisternino we love The Almond, Melting Pot Bistro, Il Cucco and Bar Pineta, and in Martina Franca, Torre del Angelico and Curcuma Pizzeria.

How do I book?

Send your dates with the form below, or call or WhatsApp us. We'll confirm availability and your price, with no platform fees.
