    Good to know

    Questions we're asked every day

    Can't see yours? Call 020 8543 0131 — you'll speak to someone who's been doing this for years.

    Do you come to my home to measure?

    Yes. We arrange an appointment to measure accurately and see the room's light, walls and furnishings — and we'll bring samples so you can see them in place before you decide.

    Which carpet is best with pets and children?

    It depends on the room and the traffic. Hard-wearing wool blends, loop-free twists and some naturals cope brilliantly; others don't. Tell us who lives with you and we'll steer you to something that looks great and lasts.

    How quickly can you fit?

    Some of our ranges are held in stock for fast fitting. For made-to-order carpets we'll give you an approximate fitting date with your quote, and plan around your timescales.

    Can you fit stair runners on curved or winding stairs?

    Absolutely — it's one of our specialities. Winders, curves and open-string stairs are cut and dressed by hand on site. Browse our recent work to see examples.

    Do you only sell carpet?

    No — we also supply and fit luxury vinyl tiles and sheet (including Karndean), naturals such as sisal, seagrass, coir and jute, and commercial flooring such as Altro.

    Do I need an appointment to visit the showroom?

    No appointment needed — just pop in during opening hours. If you're making a special trip, give us a quick call first to make sure the right person is available.
