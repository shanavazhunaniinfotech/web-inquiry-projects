Questions

Asked & answered.

Something else? Call 020 8543 0131 — you'll speak to someone who's done this for years.

Do you come to my home to measure?

Yes. We arrange a visit to measure accurately and see the room's light and furnishings — bringing samples so you can see them in place.

Which carpet is best with pets and children?

It depends on the room and traffic. Hard-wearing wool blends, tight twists and some naturals cope brilliantly; others don't. Tell us who lives with you and we'll guide you.

How quickly can you fit?

Selected ranges are held in stock for fast fitting. For made-to-order carpets we'll give you an approximate date with your quote.

Can you fit runners on curved or winding stairs?

It's one of our specialities — winders, curves and open strings are cut and dressed by hand on site.

Do you only sell carpet?

No — we also supply and fit luxury vinyl (including Karndean), naturals like sisal, seagrass, coir and jute, and commercial flooring such as Altro.

Do I need an appointment for the showroom?

No — just pop in during opening hours. If you're making a special trip, a quick call first makes sure the right person is free.
