How it's built

Shopify at the core, a custom marketplace on top

Shopify isn't multi-vendor out of the box, so a marketplace layer adds vendors, order splitting, commissions and payouts. The custom UI and SMS OTP connect to both.

Recommended for the MVP

Shopify + proven marketplace app, custom-designed

  • Fastest way to launch with reliable vendor, commission & payout logic
  • Custom theme and custom vendor dashboard UI from your references
  • SMS OTP connected to the vendor login
  • Lower build cost, easy to grow later
Option for later

Shopify + fully custom marketplace app

  • Full control of vendor logic and data
  • Best when rules become very specific (custom commissions, logistics, APIs)
  • More build time, so better after the MVP proves the model
  • The final choice depends on your design references and workflow
