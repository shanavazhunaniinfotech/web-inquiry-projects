Are your fragrances really authentic?

Yes, 100%. We source only from brand-authorized distributors and wholesalers. Every bottle is factory-sealed with its original batch code, and it's backed by our money-back authenticity guarantee.

Why are your prices lower than department stores?

We buy in large volumes and operate without department-store overheads like rent and commissions, so we can pass those savings to you. Same bottles, smarter pricing.

Do you sell testers or decants?

No. Unless clearly labeled otherwise, every product is a brand-new, full retail bottle in its original packaging.

What's the difference between EDP and EDT?

Eau de Parfum (EDP) has 15–20% perfume oil and lasts about 6–8 hours. Eau de Toilette (EDT) has 5–15% and lasts 4–6 hours, with a lighter, fresher feel. Every product page lets you pick the type and size that suit you.

How fast is shipping?

Orders placed by 2pm ET ship the same business day. Standard delivery takes 2–5 business days and is free over $59. Expedited and overnight options are available at checkout.

What is your return policy?

Return any unopened item within 30 days for a full refund. If something arrives damaged, contact us and we'll make it right right away.
