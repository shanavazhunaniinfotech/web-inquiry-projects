Good questions

Straight answers, no pressure.

Still wondering? Text us at 832-693-0123. A real person (usually Colby) will answer.

How accurate is the free home value?

Far more accurate than an online estimate. We pull recent sales from your specific neighborhood, adjust for your home's upgrades and condition, and send you a realistic price range, usually within 24 hours.

Do I have to commit to listing with you?

Not at all. The valuation and the consult are free with zero obligation. Plenty of homeowners use it just to plan ahead.

We're moving to Katy from out of state. Can you help remotely?

Absolutely, it's one of our specialties. We do live FaceTime tours, neighborhood drive-throughs on video, and can coordinate the whole purchase while you're still in your current city.

Should I sell first or buy first?

It depends on your equity, your loan and your timeline. On a 15-minute call we'll walk you through both paths, including options to buy before you sell.

How fast can you list my home?

Often within 7 to 10 days of our first walkthrough, including photography, video and prep. If you need faster, tell us. We've done it.
