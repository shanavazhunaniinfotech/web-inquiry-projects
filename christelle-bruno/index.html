Start Your Inquiry
Is there a cost to send an inquiry?

No. Inquiries are free and carry no obligation. Pricing is discussed once Christelle understands your needs, and payment is arranged separately after a service is confirmed.

Which areas do you serve for real estate?

Massachusetts and Florida, through Christelle's respective brokerage affiliations. Relocating between the two? That's a great fit.

Do I have to register to search homes?

No. You can browse listings and property details freely. An optional free account lets you save favorites and searches and get alerts for new listings and price changes.

Do you provide legal or immigration advice?

No. Ellevate Interpretation Services provides language interpretation only. We don't offer legal advice, representation or document preparation, and we review each appointment's interpreter requirements before accepting.

Can interpretation be done remotely?

Yes. Remote and in-person options are available depending on the appointment, location and confirmation of requirements.

How far in advance should I plan my event?

The earlier the better, especially for weddings and large celebrations. Share your date and we'll let you know what's possible, including day-of coordination for events coming up soon.
