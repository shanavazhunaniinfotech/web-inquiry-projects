Will it work on my skin type?

Yes. The system is made for mature skin that's normal, dry or combination, and was tested on sensitive skin. The balms adapt: they absorb fast on oilier areas and nourish drier ones.

I have sensitive skin. Will it irritate me?

We use bakuchiol instead of retinol, so there's no redness, peeling or purging. Every formula is fragrance-free and dermatologist tested. If you're very reactive, patch test for 24h first.

When will I see results?

Most women feel softer, plumper skin after the first night and see a brighter glow within 2 weeks. Fine lines and firmness improve visibly from week 4, with the best results at 8-12 weeks.

How long does one system last?

About 40 days with twice-daily use. A little goes a long way, because the balms are concentrated and low in water.

Can I use it with retinol, acids or prescriptions?

Yes. Lilaé is gentle enough to layer, but you may not need retinol at all. Using prescription treatment? Check with your doctor first.
