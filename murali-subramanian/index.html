03 · Why it's built this way

Automatic, but never wrong in public.

Species, not just "bird"Basic AI often gets wildlife wrong. Try the kingfishers above: this small free model guesses badly. The full version uses a much stronger AI for species, and anything it's unsure about is flagged.
You approve before it goes liveTags are suggestions. One click to approve, or type the right species, so a wrong name never appears on your site.
Your albums do half the workThe album a photo sits in (a park, a trip, a species group) adds its tags automatically.
Camera details for freeDate, camera and lens are read straight from your original files, so tags like the year and season need no typing.
Exact locations hiddenGPS is rounded to a wider area, so nests and sensitive animals can't be pinpointed from your site.
Two outputs, two jobsKeywords and alt text help Google and visitors search your site. Hashtags are a separate, copy-ready set for Instagram.
