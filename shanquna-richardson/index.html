Why we exist

At 18, support ends. The bills don't.

Most young people lean on family well into their twenties: a spare room, a co-signer, a call when the car breaks down. The young adults we serve are aging out of foster care, leaving unstable homes or starting over, and they're doing it alone.

We fill that gap. Not with a handout, but with a plan, a place and people who show up.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Still wondering? Ask us anything, a real person replies.

    Is my donation tax-deductible?

    Yes. Stable Futures is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 00-0000000). You'll get an emailed receipt instantly and a year-end summary every January.

    How is my money actually used?

    87¢ of every dollar goes straight to programs: rent support, apprenticeships, matched savings and coaching. Our audited financials and annual report are public.

    Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

    Anytime, in two clicks from your donor portal or by replying to any receipt email. No calls, no guilt.

    Do volunteers need experience?

    No. Mentors complete a 3-hour training and a background check, and you'll always have a staff contact. What matters most is showing up.

    Can my company match my gift?

    Many do. Search your employer on the donation page or ask HR. Matches often double your impact.

    Does it cost anything?

    Never. Every program is 100% free for young adults, funded by donors and grants.

    Who can join?

    Young adults ages 18–24 living in our area who want help with housing, work, money or support. You don't need to have aged out of foster care.

    Do I need documents to start?

    No. If you're missing an ID, birth certificate or Social Security card, getting those is one of the first things we help with.

    Is it confidential?

    Yes. What you share stays with your coach. We never share your information without your permission.

    What if I need somewhere to sleep tonight?

    Call or text 211 for emergency shelter, then reach out to us. We'll help with the next step tomorrow.
