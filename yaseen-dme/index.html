    FAQ

    Questions? We've got answers.

    Still unsure? Friendly specialists, no pressure.

    (800) 555-0142
    Do I need a prescription?

    For insurance to pay, yes. Most plans need a prescription from your doctor. Don't worry: we contact your doctor's office and get it for you. If you're paying yourself, many items (like walkers and shower chairs) don't need one.

    Does Medicare cover medical equipment?

    Medicare Part B covers durable medical equipment your doctor says is medically necessary, such as wheelchairs, hospital beds, oxygen and CPAP. You usually pay 20% of the approved amount after your yearly deductible. We'll tell you your exact cost before delivery.

    Should I rent or buy?

    Renting is best for a short time, like recovering from surgery. Buying is better for long-term needs. Some plans rent first and then you own it. We'll help you choose the option that costs you less.

    How fast can you deliver?

    Most orders are delivered and set up within 24–48 hours. For hospital discharges we often deliver the same day.

    What if my equipment breaks?

    Call our 24/7 support line. We repair or replace it, and give you a loaner if a repair takes time, so you're never without.

    Do you accept HSA or FSA cards?

    Yes. We accept HSA/FSA cards, all major credit cards and offer payment plans for larger items.
