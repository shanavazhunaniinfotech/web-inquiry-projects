    Good questions

    Do I need a prescription?

    For insurance to pay, yes. Most plans need a prescription from your doctor, and we contact your doctor's office to get it for you. If you're paying yourself, many items (like walkers and shower chairs) don't need one.

    Does Medicare cover medical equipment?

    Medicare Part B covers durable medical equipment your doctor says is medically necessary, such as wheelchairs, hospital beds, oxygen and CPAP. You usually pay 20% of the approved amount after your yearly deductible.

    Should I rent or buy?

    Rent for a short time, like recovering from surgery. Buy for long-term needs. We'll help you choose the option that costs you less.

    How fast can you deliver?

    Most orders are delivered and set up within 24–48 hours. Hospital discharges are often delivered the same day.

    What if my equipment breaks?

    Call our 24/7 line. We repair or replace it and give you a loaner if a repair takes time.

    Do you accept HSA or FSA cards?

    Yes, plus all major credit cards and payment plans for larger items.
