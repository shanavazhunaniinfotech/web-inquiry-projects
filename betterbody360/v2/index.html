How does BetterBody360 work?

Select a product and complete a brief health questionnaire. A licensed medical provider reviews your information and creates a treatment plan. If approved, a licensed U.S. pharmacy ships your medication to your home, and you keep access to your provider through your patient portal.

Is BetterBody360 available in every state?

Yes. BetterBody360 is licensed to provide care in all 50 U.S. states.

Do I need insurance?

No insurance is required. We offer transparent, out-of-pocket pricing for all products and services.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Once approved and filled, most packages arrive within 1 to 5 business days. Shipping is fast, free and discreet.

Why do I receive 3 months at a time?

Most medications show minimal results with a 1-month supply. A 3-month supply with all necessary supplies sets you up for better long-term outcomes.
