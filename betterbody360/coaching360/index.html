What is Coaching360?

Coaching360 is a health and wellness program that combines in-depth health analysis, tailored recommendations, one-on-one coaching and the Coaching360 Health App to help you look better, feel better and live longer.

How much does it cost?

The Coaching360 Weight Loss Program is $299 per month and the Guaranteed Weight Loss Program is $499 per month. Both are six-month programs.

How does the guarantee work?

With the Guaranteed Weight Loss Program, you and your coach agree on a goal. If you don’t reach it, we continue your coaching at no additional cost until you do.

Which apps does the health app work with?

The Coaching360 Health App integrates with 50+ health and wellness apps, including Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, Oura and more.

Can I use Coaching360 with BetterBody360 products?

Yes. Your personalized plan can include clinically guided products alongside nutrition, exercise and lifestyle strategies.
