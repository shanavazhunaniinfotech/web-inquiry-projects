Brand Partner FAQs

Brand Partner questions, answered.

Answers to common questions about the Brand Partner program.

What is a Brand Partner?

Brand Partners market BetterBody360’s health and wellness products and programs through their personalized website and earn commissions when customers make a purchase.

Who makes a great Brand Partner?

Health and wellness professionals, coaches, trainers, med spas, influencers and anyone with an audience interested in better health. No special license or medical certification is required.

How much does it cost?

A $199 setup fee applies if approved, plus a $99 monthly fee. There are no technology or doctor fees, no long-term commitment, and you can cancel anytime.

What does BetterBody360 handle?

Licensed providers in all 50 states, clinical oversight and prescriptions, pharmacy fulfillment and delivery, telehealth infrastructure, and customer and partner support.

What do I do as a Brand Partner?

Share your website with your audience. Every order from your website is automatically credited to your account, and you can track referrals and orders in your Brand Partner dashboard.

How do Brand Partners earn income?

Monthly commissions of 20% of sales are paid on the 15th of each month, plus a bonus for referring other Brand Partners.
