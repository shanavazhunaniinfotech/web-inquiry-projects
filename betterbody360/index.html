How does BetterBody360 work?

Select a product and complete a brief health questionnaire. A licensed medical provider reviews your information and creates a treatment plan. If approved, a licensed U.S. pharmacy ships your medication immediately, and you keep access to your provider through your patient portal.

Is pricing all-inclusive?

Yes. There are no doctor’s fees, no membership fees and no shipping fees. The price you see includes your provider review, prescription and shipping.

Do I need insurance?

No insurance is required. We offer transparent, out-of-pocket pricing for all products and services.

How fast will my order ship?

Once approved, your order is shipped immediately from a licensed U.S. pharmacy. Shipping is always free and discreet.

Is BetterBody360 available in every state?

Yes. BetterBody360 is licensed to provide care in all 50 U.S. states.
