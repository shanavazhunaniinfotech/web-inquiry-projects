How does BetterBody360 work?

After selecting a product and completing a brief health questionnaire, a licensed medical provider reviews your information and creates a treatment plan designed for you. If approved, your medication is dispensed by a licensed U.S. pharmacy and shipped to your home, and you keep access to your provider through your patient portal.

Is BetterBody360 available in every state?

Yes. BetterBody360 is licensed to provide care in all 50 U.S. states, with a network of licensed medical providers and pharmacy partners serving patients nationwide.

Do I need insurance?

No insurance is required. We offer transparent, out-of-pocket pricing for all of our products and services.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Once your order is approved and filled at the pharmacy, most packages arrive within 1 to 5 business days. Shipping is fast, free and discreet, and you’ll get a tracking link by email.

Why do I receive 3 months at a time?

Most medications show no to minimal results with a 1-month supply. A 3-month ongoing supply of medication and all necessary supplies sets you up for better outcomes and long-term success.

Who can access my health information?

Only you, BetterBody360 and your assigned medical provider. We never sell, rent or share your personal data for marketing, and all PHI is encrypted at rest and in transit in line with HIPAA.
