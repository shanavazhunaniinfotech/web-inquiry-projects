      08Questions

      Where do you deliver, and is it free?

      Free local delivery covers Denver Metro and within 20 miles of Longmont, and most orders arrive within 5 days. Everywhere else gets flat-rate shipping at checkout.

      How does the multi-buy discount work?

      Mix any jars you like. 3 jars save 10%, 6 save 15% and 12 save 20%. It's applied in your basket automatically, no code needed.

      How long do the jars keep?

      Unopened jars keep well in a cool, dark cupboard. Once opened, keep them in the fridge. Ferments keep slowly developing flavour.

      Are they really preservative free?

      Yes. No additives or preservatives, ever. Every ingredient is listed on the jar and the product page.

      Can my shop or restaurant stock your jars?

      We'd love that. 20+ Colorado shops, delis and restaurants already carry us. Message us for wholesale pricing and food-service sizes.
