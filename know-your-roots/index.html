Know your roots

We keep the business in the family.

It started the simple way: a kitchen, a crock and a farmers market table. Lesley, a mom, wife and accountant who cooks to unwind, found her happy place at the markets. One jar at a time, regulars became friends and friends became family.

Today the whole Mortimer family is in on it. We still pack every jar by hand, still pick up veg from small local farms, and still stand behind the table most weekends. If you've met us at a market, you already know the deal: we will never put profit ahead of quality.

    Good questions

    Before you stock up

    Can't see your question? Message us on Instagram, we reply fast.

    Where do you deliver, and is it free?

    Free local delivery covers Denver Metro and within 20 miles of Longmont, and most orders arrive within 5 days. Use the ZIP checker above to see if you qualify. Everywhere else gets flat-rate shipping at checkout.

    How does the multi-buy discount work?

    Mix any jars you like. 3 jars save 10%, 6 save 15% and 12 save 20%. It's applied in your cart automatically, no code needed.

    How long do the jars keep?

    Unopened jars keep well in a cool, dark cupboard. Once opened, keep them in the fridge. Ferments keep slowly developing flavour, which is half the fun.

    Are your pickles really preservative free?

    Yes. No additives or preservatives, ever. Just veg, salt, vinegar or brine, herbs and spices. Every ingredient is listed on the jar and the product page.

    I own a shop or restaurant. Can I stock your jars?

    We'd love that. 20+ Colorado shops, delis and restaurants already carry us. Message us for wholesale pricing and food-service sizes.
