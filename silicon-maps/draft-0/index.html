Who actually sees the maps?

75,000+ prints go out every year through trade shows, direct mail and participating companies to CEOs, VCs, engineers, researchers, government and economic development leaders, and university leaders. Sponsors also hand them out internally to sales, marketing, HR and their executives.

When is the deadline for the 2027 edition?

Spaces are first come, first served and limited on every map. Artwork is finalized in autumn, when every sponsor gets a link to approve their final placement. The earlier you book, the more location options you have.

What artwork do I need to provide?

A vector logo (.AI or .EPS), a high-resolution JPEG of your building, and any brand guidelines. Our art department designs your ad space and custom imprint and sends you a proof.

What's the difference between the interactive logo and an Expanded Profile?

Every package includes an interactive logo that enlarges on hover and links to your website. An Expanded Profile (Gold and up) adds contact details, a company summary, social links, product and event photos, careers and investor links, and an intro video.

We're a startup. Is this for us?

Yes. Participants range from companies building their first product to Fortune 100 multinationals. Appearing next to industry leaders is the point: it puts you in the same frame.

Can we pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Reserve your spot and we'll send an invoice, or pay by card at checkout.
