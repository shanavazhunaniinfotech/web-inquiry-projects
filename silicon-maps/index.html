    Questions

    Straight answers.

    SM Talk to the map makers925-314-1130, ext. 11 · sales@siliconmaps.com
    Who actually sees the maps?

    75,000+ prints go out each year through trade shows, direct mail and the companies on the map: CEOs, VCs, engineers, researchers, government and economic development leaders, and universities. Sponsors also hand them out to their own sales, marketing and HR teams and their executives.

    When is the 2027 deadline?

    Spaces are first come, first served and limited on every map. Artwork is finalized in autumn, when every sponsor gets a link to approve their final placement. The earlier you book, the more location options you have.

    What artwork do I need?

    A vector logo (.AI or .EPS), a high-resolution JPEG of your building, and any brand guidelines. Our art department designs your ad space and imprint and sends you a proof.

    Interactive logo vs. Expanded Profile?

    Every package includes an interactive logo that enlarges on hover and links to your website. The Expanded Profile (Gold and up) adds contact details, a company summary, social links, product and event photos, careers and investor links, and an intro video.

    We're a startup. Is it for us?

    Yes. Participants range from companies building their first product to Fortune 100 multinationals. Sharing the map with industry leaders is exactly the point.

    Can we pay by invoice or PO?

    Yes. Reserve now and we'll send an invoice, or pay by card.
