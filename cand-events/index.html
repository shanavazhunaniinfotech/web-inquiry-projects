Questions, answered.

What's the minimum order?

Our minimum is 24 cans, and there's no real maximum. We produce orders in the thousands, with capacity for 100,000+. Cans are sold in cartons of 24 (24, 48, 72 and so on).

How much does it cost?

$6.50 per can for 24 cans, with a better price per can and a personalised quote for bulk orders.

How long does it take?

Turnaround is 2–3 weeks, depending on volume. We'll confirm timing in your quote.

What's in the can?

Still water, in a 330ml sleek aluminium can.

How is my logo printed?

Your design is UV printed straight onto the can. No stickers, no sleeves, no peeling. Your logo prints up to 50 × 50 mm on the front of the can.

What file do you need?

A PNG with a transparent background (no white box), at least 2000 px wide or 300 dpi. You can paste a Canva, Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link in your enquiry.

Will I see it before it's printed?

Yes. We send a digital mockup first. Nothing prints until you approve it.

Do you deliver interstate?

Yes. Include your postcode in your enquiry and we'll confirm delivery timing for Sydney or interstate.
