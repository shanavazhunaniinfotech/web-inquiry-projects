01 — Good to know

TIMING, EXPLAINED

Why a missed period?

hCG is produced after a fertilised egg implants. By the first day of a missed period, Surecheck detects it with over 99% accuracy.

Any time of day

No need to wait for the morning. Just avoid drinking a lot of liquid right before testing.

Irregular cycle?

Test 19 days after you think you may have conceived. If the result is negative, test again after 48 hours.

Negative, no period?

Test again after 48 hours. If your period is very late or you're worried, talk to your doctor.

03 — FAQs

TIMING QUESTIONS

See all FAQs
I have an irregular period. When should I test?

If your period is irregular, you can use Surecheck® 19 days after you think you may have conceived. If the result is negative, test again after 48 hours.

My result was negative but my period hasn't started.

Test again after 48 hours. If your period is very late or you are worried, talk to your doctor.

I tested "Pregnant", but later got "Not pregnant" or my period started. What does this mean?

Surecheck is over 99% accurate from the first day of a missed period. A positive result, even a pale second line, means you were pregnant. Natural loss in the first weeks of pregnancy, known as early pregnancy loss, is unfortunately not uncommon. Please talk to your doctor.

When can I use the test?

Surecheck® can be used from the first day of a missed period, at any time of the day.

Do I need to use first-morning urine?

No. Surecheck can be used at any time of the day. Avoid drinking large amounts of liquid just before testing, as it can dilute hCG in your urine.
