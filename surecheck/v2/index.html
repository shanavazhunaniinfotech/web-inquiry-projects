07 — FAQs

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? If in doubt about your result, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.

See all FAQs
01When can I use the test?

Surecheck® can be used from the first day of a missed period, at any time of the day.

02What if the line in the test region (T) is very pale?

A pale second line means you are pregnant. Thanks to Colloidal Gold technology, even a faint line is a valid result.

03I have an irregular period. When should I test?

If your period is irregular, you can use Surecheck® 19 days after you think you may have conceived. If the result is negative, test again after 48 hours.

04What can affect the result?

Certain medications can affect the result. Within 8 weeks after childbirth or abortion, a false positive is possible because hCG can stay in the body. Drinking a lot of liquid can dilute hCG in urine. Birth control pills, antibiotics, painkillers and alcohol do not affect the result.

05Can I read the result after 10 minutes?

No. Ignore any result read after the 10th minute.

06A pink background or streaks appeared in the window. Is that a problem?

No. Differences in urine composition and room humidity can cause a pink background or vertical streaks. They do not affect the result.
