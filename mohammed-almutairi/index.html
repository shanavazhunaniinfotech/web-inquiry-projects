How it's built

Shopify does commerce.
We build the marketplace layer.

Shopify doesn't support multiple vendors out of the box. The usual approach, and the one I'd use here, is to keep Shopify for products, checkout and payments, and add a custom marketplace app on top for vendors, OTP login and commissions.

What people see

Marketplace storefront

Your custom UI, built from your design references: home, categories, search, product pages, vendor pages and cart.

Custom Shopify theme (Liquid) or headless (Hydrogen)

Vendor dashboard

A separate web app for vendors. Vendors don't need Shopify accounts or staff seats.

React web app, SMS OTP login

Admin panel

Vendor and product approvals, commissions and payouts. Can live inside your Shopify admin as an embedded app.

Embedded Shopify app

Marketplace app (the custom part)

A secure backend that connects everything. It links each product and order line to a vendor, splits multi-vendor orders, and keeps every vendor's records separate.

Vendor accountsSMS OTP authProduct ↔ vendor mappingOrder splittingCommission enginePayout ledgerWebhooks & notifications

Shopify

Three ways to build it

Which approach fits your MVP?

There's more than one way to build a Shopify marketplace. Here's an honest comparison so we can pick the right one on our call.

A · Ready-made marketplace app

A Shopify marketplace app from the App Store, plus theme customisation.

Speed to launch
Custom UI freedom
SMS OTP vendor login
Low monthly fees
  • +Fastest way to start
  • –The vendor dashboard looks and works the way the app decides
  • –OTP login and custom flows are usually not possible
Recommended for MVP

B · Custom theme + custom app

Your design built as a Shopify theme, plus our own marketplace app and vendor dashboard.

Speed to launch
Custom UI freedom
SMS OTP vendor login
Low monthly fees
  • +Matches your designs and flows exactly
  • +You own the code, with no per-vendor fees
  • +Can move to headless later without rebuilding the backend

C · Fully headless

A Hydrogen / React front end using Shopify's Storefront API, plus the same custom app.

Speed to launch
Custom UI freedom
SMS OTP vendor login
Low monthly fees
  • +App-like speed and full design freedom
  • –Longer build and more to maintain
  • –Usually better for phase 2 than for the MVP
