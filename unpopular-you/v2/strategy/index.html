Design rationale · v2 "Sensory Editorial"

Premium enough to win trust. Built to convert.

Paid Meta traffic, ~70–80% mobile, mostly cold, converting under 1%. Every section answers one question: does this help a first-time visitor on a phone understand, trust and buy — fast? The structure follows your Pre-Scale CRO Audit section by section; the look follows your brand guidelines and the references you love (Rhode, Summer Fridays, Topicals, POOM).

What makes it feel premium

A
Brand

Your palette, your wordmark, editorial type

Vanilla Mist, Porcelain, Rosy Clay, Clay Taupe, Rose Dust pink and Cocoa — straight from your guidelines. Headlines pair Jost (it matches your logo) with an elegant italic serif for warmth. A soft film grain gives the whole site a tactile, beauty-editorial feel.

B
Product

Your products, cut out and floating

We cut every product out of your photos so they float over soft brand tints instead of grey studio backgrounds — the Rhode / Summer Fridays look. Each step has its own tint, so the routine is recognisable at a glance.

C
Motion

Award-style motion that guides, not distracts

Smooth scrolling, a short branded intro (first visit only), headlines that rise word by word, a pinned 4-step routine story, a barrier "wall" that builds itself, a drag-to-compare traditions slider, a cursor-reveal ingredient lens, a swipeable review deck, products that assemble into the bundle, and items that fly into the bag. All motion switches off for people who prefer reduced motion, and nothing auto-plays sound.

D
Trust

Real people with melanin-rich skin, everywhere

Your own unboxing videos and founder imagery, plus placeholder lifestyle photography of melanin-rich skin (to be replaced by your shoots). Cold visitors need to see themselves on the site in the first second.

Home page, in order

01
Audit #01 · #04

Announcement + hero

"Skincare for melanin-rich skin that glows" names who it's for and the outcome; the subheading says what's different. One primary CTA ("Shop the routine") plus a softer path for undecided visitors ("Take the 60-sec skin quiz"). Star rating with real faces, then Nurse-founded · Made for melanin-rich skin · 20,000+ community. No sale in the hero; the bar carries only real announcements and rotates slowly.

02
Audit #05

Press as quotes

One publication at a time with what they said, led by Blissim's real line. Tap or swipe; never auto-moving.

03
Philosophy

Barrier First, made visual

The strongest objection-handler for this audience: on melanin-rich skin, irritation can cause more dark marks. A side-by-side "typical brightening vs barrier first" plus a skin-barrier wall that rebuilds itself (cells + ceramide "mortar") makes the philosophy instantly understood — and makes gentle products feel like the smarter choice, not the weaker one.

04
Audit #06

Two traditions — drag to compare

Jamaican botanicals on one side, Korean-inspired science on the other, joined by a "Barrier first" handle. Interaction = attention: people who drag remember the difference no bigger brand can copy.

05
Audit #07 · #09

Values band + the pinned Core Four story

Instead of a thin bestseller grid, the four products are told as one routine: the section pins while you scroll through 01 Cleanse → 02 Prep → 03 Correct → 04 Seal, each with its one-liner, ingredients, rating, price and Add to bag. On mobile it becomes stacked cards you swipe through. A "All four for $118" CTA is always visible.

06
Audit #08 · Quiz

Find your first step + skin quiz

Two speeds: tap a concern for an instant recommendation with Add to bag, or take the 4-question quiz. The quiz ends in a personalised duo or full ritual with a discount, and asks for an email in exchange for 20% off (skippable). It's both a conversion tool and your best email-capture engine — far stronger than a popup.

07
Audit #19

Ingredients you can feel

A cursor "lens" reveals the texture beneath — the premium version of the old clipart infographics. Each ingredient says what it does and which product it's in.

08
Audit #11

Real-life videos

Your unboxing clips, tap to play, captioned, each linked to its product. A "Share your routine" card invites UGC from your 20K community.

09
Audit #10 · #12 · #13

Founder → results → reviews

Shauna the nurse is the trust; the quote lights up word by word as you read. Results are labelled as a consumer study with the source line (FTC/ASA safe). Reviews are a swipeable deck — one specific review at a time with name, skin concern and a product link — stronger than exposing a low review count.

10
Audit #14 · #24

Bundle, FAQ, final CTA

The bundle leads with the problem it solves, then the $24 saving; products assemble into the set as you scroll. The FAQ handles the 8 cold-buyer objections. A final full-width CTA catches people who scrolled to the bottom.

Collection, product page & cart

C
Audit #21 · AOV

Collection with build-your-routine

Borderless cards, same shape, tinted per step; hover shows the product in real life (video where we have it). Core Four leads as a feature card. "+ Routine" on each card fills a floating tray: 2 steps = 10% off, 3 = 15%, 4 = Core Four price — the bundle builder you mentioned on our call. A "match your concern" table answers "which do I need?"

P
Audit #15–#20

Product page in buying order

Gallery (cut-out hero with subtle 3D tilt, lifestyle, texture, video) → rating → title → one-liner → price + size → four tick bullets → One-time / Subscribe & save 15% / Upgrade to Core Four → Add to bag → shipping · secure · guarantee → payment icons → 20% signup → values → accordions. Below: orbiting ingredient spotlight, "What to expect" 8-week timeline, Complete the Routine ("You're here"), FAQ + video, reviews, related. Sticky Add to bag on mobile.

B
Audit #22

The bag

Free-shipping progress to $60, live nudges ("Add 1 more step to save 15%"), automatic routine discount, a clear "You're saving" line, Complete-your-routine upsell, payment icons. No floating popups anywhere.
