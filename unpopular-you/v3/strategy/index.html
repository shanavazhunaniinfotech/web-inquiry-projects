Version A vs B

How this version differs

A
Look

Bright bento tiles, bold caps

White base, porcelain and Rose Dust tiles, generous rounding and one typeface (Jost, which matches your wordmark) in confident uppercase, like the "EFFORTLESSLY UNIQUELY YOU" headings in your guidelines. Calmer and more clinical-modern than the editorial Version A.

B
Hero

A bento hero, everything above the fold

One glance shows the headline + one CTA + trust points, a real person with melanin-rich skin applying the product, a rotating product tile and a 4.9★ proof tile with a real review. On mobile the headline and button come first, so the buy path is the first thing a thumb sees.

C
Signature moments

Different interactions, same goals

A Barrier First statement that fills in as you read, with layered skin rings (Protect · Calm · Correct). The Core Four as a sideways-scrolling gallery. Expanding panels for the two traditions. Flip cards for ingredients. A video grid that previews on hover. A review rail you can drag.

D
Collection

Sidebar shopping with a live routine builder

Concern filters and sort sit in a sticky sidebar, next to an always-visible "Build your routine" panel: 2 steps = −10%, 3 = −15%, 4 = Core Four price. On phones it becomes a bottom tray. The Core Four leads as a full-width feature card.

E
Product page

Slider gallery, compact buy box, tabs

A swipeable gallery with thumbnails; the buy box keeps only what drives the decision (rating, title, one-liner, price + size, 4 benefit tiles, One-time / Subscribe −15% / Core Four, Add to bag, trust, payment, 20% offer). Details move into tabs below, followed by an ingredient bento, an 8-week timeline, Complete the Routine, FAQ, reviews and related products. Sticky Add to bag on mobile.
