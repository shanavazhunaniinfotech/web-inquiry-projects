Our story

Made by a nurse, for skin like ours

Unpopular You is barrier-first skincare for melanin-rich skin — gentle formulas that bring Jamaican botanicals and Korean-inspired skin science together.

Unpopular You started with one frustration. Melanin-rich skin was either treated with products too harsh to tolerate, or left out of the conversation altogether.

Shauna, a nurse, spent years dealing with acne and acne scars and couldn't find skincare that worked without making her skin worse. So she made her own.

“As a nurse, skin health comes first for me. I wanted products that treat dark spots and sensitivity gently — made for skin like mine, and like yours.”

That's where Barrier First comes from. On melanin-rich skin, irritation can leave new dark marks behind, so every Unpopular You formula protects your barrier first — then works gently on dark spots, dullness and uneven tone.
