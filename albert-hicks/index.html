How fast will I see results?

Your first content goes live within 14 days. Most clients see clear engagement gains in the first month, and paid campaigns usually start producing leads in weeks 2–4. Organic growth compounds over 3–6 months.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month after the first month. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Your ad budget goes straight to the platforms (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn) from your own account, so you always own the data and see every dollar. Our fee covers strategy, creative and management.

How much of my time will this take?

About 1 hour a month: a short approval of the content calendar and a monthly results call. We handle everything else.

Do you work with businesses in my industry?

We've grown brands in retail, e-commerce, restaurants, fitness, health and B2B services. On the call we'll share examples closest to your industry.

Who owns the content and accounts?

You do, always. Every photo, video, ad account and follower stays yours, even if we stop working together.

What happens on the strategy call?

We look at your current accounts, your competitors and your goals, then show you 2–3 concrete opportunities. You'll leave with a plan whether or not you hire us.
