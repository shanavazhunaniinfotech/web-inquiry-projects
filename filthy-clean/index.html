FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I use it?

Remove the excess stain, twist the base to dispense the formula onto the tip, then dab. Don't stab or scrub. Cap it and let it dry.

What stains is it for?

It's an instant stain remover built for everyday mess: wine, coffee, sauce, berries and makeup. Dab it on as soon as it happens for the best result.

Which fabrics can I use it on?

It's plant-powered and made without harsh chemicals. On delicate fabrics, test a hidden spot first, as you would with any stain remover.

What's in it?

A plant-powered formula, made without bleach, parabens, sulfates (SLS/SLES), optical brighteners or harsh solvents.

What do the scents smell like?

Lethal Linen is fresh and airy, like clean sheets with a bite. Sultry Santal is warm and woody, creamy sandalwood with a moody edge. Neither smells like a cleaner.
