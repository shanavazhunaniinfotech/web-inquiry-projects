            WhereSettingOptions
            Theme settingsLogoImage picker + width slider. Works in portrait and landscape.
            Theme settingsColours4 schemes (background, text, accent, button), each with a matching foreground.
            Theme settingsTypographyHeading and body font pickers (default Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans), heading scale.
            Theme settingsCorner styleSquare, Editorial (2px, default) or Soft. The arch frame stays as the signature shape.
            Theme settingsProduct cardsSecond image on hover, show metal line, show quick add, badge position.
            Theme settingsCartDrawer or page, cart upsell on/off and source collection, free-shipping threshold message.
            HeaderLayoutLogo centred (default) or logo left; sticky on/off; mega menu with promo image.
            HeaderAnnouncementText, link, country and language selectors on/off.
            Main productBlocksTitle, price, variant picker (swatches or pills), engraving field, quantity, buy buttons, pickup, USP list, collapsible tabs, share, @app and Custom Liquid.
            Main productSticky add to cartOn/off. Appears once the main button scrolls out of view.
