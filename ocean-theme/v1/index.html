      01Is your jewellery solid gold or plated?

      Our fine pieces are solid 14k or 18k recycled gold. Our everyday pieces are gold vermeil: a thick layer of gold over sterling silver. Each product page shows which it is.

      02Is it safe for sensitive skin?

      Yes. Everything is nickel-free and hypoallergenic. Our earring posts are solid gold or sterling silver.

      03Can I add engraving?

      Free engraving is available on rings and pendants, up to 12 characters. Engraved pieces can still be resized, but can’t be returned.

      04How long does delivery take?

      Orders ship within 2 working days. Express delivery is free on orders over $100.00 and arrives in 1–2 working days.

      05What if my ring doesn’t fit?

      Resizing is free within 60 days. Start a request from your account and we’ll send a prepaid label.

      06Do you ship internationally?

      We ship to 32 countries. Prices, duties and taxes are shown in your local currency at checkout.
