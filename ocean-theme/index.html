      Questions

      Can’t find an answer? Contact our team. We reply within one business day.

      Is everything solid gold?

      Yes. Every piece is solid recycled 14k gold, stamped 585. We don’t make plated or vermeil jewellery.

      Are your diamonds real?

      Our diamonds are lab-grown: chemically identical to mined diamonds, graded by IGI, with reports included above 0.3 ct.

      What if my ring doesn’t fit?

      Resizing is free within 60 days, including engraved rings. Start a request from your account and we’ll send a prepaid label.

      How long does delivery take?

      Orders ship insured within 2 working days and usually arrive 2–3 working days later. Shipping is free over $100.00.

      Do you ship internationally?

      We ship to 32 countries. Prices, duties and taxes are shown in your local currency at checkout.
