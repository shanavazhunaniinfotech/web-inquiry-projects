You stopped the pill. Your chin noticed.

For years, the pill kept your hormones on a steady, predictable line. When you stop, your body has to find its own rhythm again, and for a lot of women that rebound shows up in one very specific place: the chin and jawline.

These aren't the little whiteheads you got as a teenager. They're deeper, tender bumps that sit under the skin for weeks. You change your cleanser, add a serum, try a spot treatment. Nothing really sticks.

That's because creams work on the surface, and post-pill acne usually doesn't start there.

What's actually going on

Three things that tend to happen after the pill.

  1. 1

    Hormones rebound

    Without the pill holding things steady, your natural hormone levels swing while your body recalibrates.

  2. 2

    Estrogen builds up

    Your liver is in charge of clearing used estrogen. When it can't keep up, the balance tips.

  3. 3

    Oil goes into overdrive

    That imbalance can push oil glands along the chin and jaw to work harder, which is where breakouts cluster.
