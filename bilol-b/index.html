04 · Two ways to fix it

Keep your site, or move to WooCommerce.

A · Keep this site

Add the missing back end

  • Orders and contact messages go to your Telegram and are saved safely online
  • A real admin login, so only you can change products and prices
  • Price changes show for every customer, not only on your device
  • Payme and Click payments connected

Best if: you want to keep the exact design you built and launch fast.

B · WordPress + WooCommerce

A full store system

  • Orders, stock, customers and discounts built in
  • Payme and Click both offer WooCommerce plugins
  • Telegram message for every new order
  • Your current design and Uzbek text carried over

Best if: you will add many products and want to manage everything yourself for years.

Your hosting question

Netlify is not what stops the orders. It is fine for the site as it is now, and Vercel or Cloudflare Pages are good free options too. What's missing is a back end that receives orders. If you choose WooCommerce (option B), use WordPress hosting such as Hostinger or SiteGround.

Fix first: today's two problems

Before anything else, the order problem and the visible admin password should be fixed. That can be a small fixed-price first step, and the rest can follow once you choose A or B.
