Ask on WhatsApp
Can non-residents get a mortgage in Barbados?

Yes. Barbados lenders do offer mortgages to non-residents, although the criteria differ from those for residents, and a larger deposit and additional documentation are usually required. We explain what applies to your country of residence and income before you start searching.

Why speak to an adviser before I find a property?

Knowing your realistic budget, deposit and timeline in advance means you view the right properties, negotiate from a position of strength and avoid delays in financing once you have found the right home.

How much deposit will I need?

It depends on residency, the property and the lender. Non-resident and luxury purchases often need a larger deposit. We will give you a realistic range during your consultation.

Are you a lender? Can you guarantee approval?

No. Tropical Finance Broker is an advisory firm, not a lender. We engage with banks and institutions on your behalf, and every mortgage remains subject to the lender’s own approval and valuation.

What does a consultation cost?

The 20-minute introductory consultation is complimentary. A one-hour Mortgage Planning Consultation is US$150, and commercial and specialist advisory is priced by scope. Standard brokerage services are often provided at no direct cost to you.

Can I borrow in US dollars?

Foreign-currency mortgage options may be available, depending on the lender and your circumstances. We help you weigh USD against BBD borrowing in relation to your income currency.

Do I need to send financial documents to enquire?

No. Your first enquiry needs only basic details. If we proceed, we will tell you exactly which documents are needed and how to share them securely.

Can we meet remotely?

Yes. Consultations are available by Zoom, WhatsApp or phone, or in person at Summerland House, Prospect Road, St James.
