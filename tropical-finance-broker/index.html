For international buyers

Buying in Barbados from overseas?

Many of our clients live in North America and the UK. Here is what typically matters most when you finance from abroad.

  • Freehold ownershipNon-residents can generally purchase Barbados property freehold.
  • Stable currencyThe Barbados dollar has been pegged at BBD 2 : USD 1 since 1975.
  • Register your fundsForeign funds brought in for a purchase are registered with the Central Bank so they can be repatriated later.
  • Advice across time zonesBarbados is on AST (UTC−4). Zoom consultations fit around New York, Toronto and London hours.

US buyers financing in Barbados

  • Lenders usually look for a larger deposit from non-residents than from local buyers
  • Income is evidenced with tax returns, W-2s or 1099s, plus a credit report
  • USD-denominated mortgage options may be available, which avoids currency mismatch
  • We coordinate with your Barbados attorney on timing and completion

Can non-residents get a mortgage in Barbados?

Yes. Barbados lenders do offer mortgages to non-residents, although criteria differ from those for residents. A larger deposit and additional documentation are usually required. We explain what applies to your country of residence and income before you start searching.

Why speak to an adviser before I find a property?

Knowing your realistic budget, deposit and timeline in advance means you view the right properties, negotiate from a position of strength and avoid losing a home to delays in financing.

How much deposit will I need?

It depends on residency, the property and the lender. Non-resident and luxury purchases often need a larger deposit. We will give you a realistic range during your consultation.

Are you a bank? Can you guarantee approval?

No. Tropical Finance Broker is an independent advisory firm, not a lender. We prepare and present your application to suitable lenders. Every mortgage remains subject to the lender’s own approval and valuation.

What does a consultation cost?

Your 20-minute introductory consultation is complimentary. A one-hour Mortgage Planning Consultation is US$150. Commercial and specialist work is quoted by scope. Standard brokerage is often at no direct cost to you, and any fee is confirmed in writing before work begins.

Can I borrow in US dollars?

Foreign-currency mortgage options may be available depending on the lender and your circumstances. We help you weigh USD against BBD borrowing in relation to your income currency.

Do I need to send financial documents to enquire?

No. Your first enquiry only needs basic details. If we proceed, we will tell you exactly which documents are needed and how to share them securely.

Can we meet remotely?

Yes. Consultations are available by Zoom, WhatsApp or phone, or in person at our office at Summerland House, Prospect Road, St James.
