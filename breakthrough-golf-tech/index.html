The science of square

Why a shaft decides where the ball starts.

01

Steel twists under load

Off-centre hits and an imperfect stroke put torque on the shaft. A steel shaft lets the head rotate — the toe and heel fall out of sync and the face opens or closes.

02

Eight layers fight back

Our patented multi-material build wraps eight layers of high-modulus carbon fibre around a stainless-steel tip. Flexural rigidity goes up, torsional rotation goes down.

03

The face arrives square

Toe and heel keep their relationship through the stroke. Fewer start-line misses, more confident short putts — with the putter you already love.

Our story

From the founder of Adams Golf, a new obsession.

Barney Adams spent a career changing what golfers expect from their clubs. In a suburb of Dallas, he started again with one question: what if the shaft was the problem?

Breakthrough Golf Technology began with the Stability putter shaft, then the Paradox zero-torque putter, BRAVA and now FORTA wood shafts — each one built to keep the club face where you aimed it.
