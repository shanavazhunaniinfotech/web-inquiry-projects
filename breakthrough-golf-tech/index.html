Steel was never designed for putting.

The conventional steel putter shaft is a century-old design. Under the forces of a real stroke — and especially an off-centre strike — it flexes and oscillates, letting the face twist away from your target line.

  • 100-year-old design
  • Oscillates mid-stroke
02

Stability: ultra-stiff, patented, multi-material.

A full composite body over a steel tip makes Stability dramatically stiffer and more consistent than steel. Toe and heel stay in sync, so the face arrives square and your putt starts on line.

  • Patented
  • 2 years of R&D
  • Fits most putters
03

Paradox: Swing Balance™ takes it further.

Paradox integrates the shaft, head weight distribution and the golfer into one patented system. Its Principal Axis stays aligned through the stroke — so you stop making little corrections.

  • Principal Axis alignment
  • Low heel · high toe

Will a Stability shaft fit my putter?+

Stability Tour fits most putters. Stability ONE fits straight-shaft and bucket-hosel designs with .370" or .355" tips (not over-nub hosels). Not sure? Tell us your brand and model at checkout and we'll confirm before we build.

How fast is installation and shipping?+

Installations are typically turned around within 24 hours of receiving your putter, and orders typically ship 24–48 hours from receipt. Send clubs to: Breakthrough Golf Technology, Attn: Orders, 1850 N Greenville Ave Ste 184, Richardson, TX 75081.

Can I adjust lie, loft and length on Paradox?+

Yes — Paradox Tour (Stability Tour shaft) adjusts lie ±3° and loft ±2° from standard 70°/3°, with lengths from 33" to 38" in ½" steps. The single-piece Paradox graphite shaft is not lie/loft adjustable.

Which Forta flex and adapter should I choose?+

Forta comes in A, R, S and X flex for drivers and 3, 5, 7 or 9 woods. We can install your OEM adapter (Callaway, Cobra, Mizuno, Ping, PXG, TaylorMade, Titleist) and grip so it's ready to play.

Which Silo Tour weight is right for me?+

90 g is lighter and more responsive, 115 g is balanced, and 130 g offers maximum control. Order two or more and save — down to $179.99 per shaft for three or more.

Are the tour pros paid to use BGT?+

No. 140+ professionals across the PGA TOUR, LIV, Korn Ferry, LPGA/Epson, Champions and mini tours have BGT in play — without compensation.

How do I avoid counterfeit shafts?+

Buy direct from BGT or an authorized dealer. Use our dealer locator, or contact us to verify a seller.
