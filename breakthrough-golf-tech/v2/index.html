FAQ

Before
you buy.

Still unsure? Call a fitter at 972-972-8882 or email info@bgolftech.com.

Will a Stability shaft fit my putter?

Stability Tour fits most putters. Stability ONE fits straight-shaft and bucket-hosel designs with .370" or .355" tips (not over-nub hosels). Tell us your brand and model at checkout and we'll confirm before we build.

How fast is installation and shipping?

Installations are typically turned around within 24 hours of receiving your putter, and orders typically ship 24–48 hours from receipt.

Can I adjust lie, loft and length on Paradox?

Yes — Paradox Tour adjusts lie ±3° and loft ±2° from standard 70° / 3°, in lengths from 33" to 38". The single-piece graphite shaft isn't lie/loft adjustable.

Which Forta flex and adapter should I choose?

Forta comes in A, R, S and X flex for drivers and 3, 5, 7 or 9 woods. We can install your OEM adapter (Callaway, Cobra, Mizuno, Ping, PXG, TaylorMade, Titleist) and grip.

Which Silo Tour weight is right for me?

90 g is lighter and more responsive, 115 g is balanced, and 130 g offers maximum control. Buy two or more and save — down to $179.99 each for three or more.

Are the tour pros paid to use BGT?

No. 140+ professionals across the PGA TOUR, LIV, Korn Ferry, LPGA / Epson, Champions and mini tours have BGT in play — without compensation.
