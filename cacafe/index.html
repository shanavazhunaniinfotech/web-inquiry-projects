Our story

From a doctor's kitchen to your morning.

Our founder, Dr. Ted Chao, trained in medicine and specialized in liver health before his family moved to the U.S. in 1999. In his Southern California kitchen he started blending herbal drinks that tasted as good as they made you feel.

After more than a decade of prototyping, CAcafé launched in 2012 on one simple idea: the best daily habits are the ones you look forward to.

How do I prepare it?

Add one to two tablespoons to 8 oz of hot or iced water or plant milk and stir. It dissolves in seconds with no frother needed. It also works in smoothies.

How much caffeine is in a cup?

Our Weight Management Coffee has about 45–50mg per serving, roughly half a standard brewed coffee, for a steady lift without the jitters.

Is CAcafé dairy-free or vegan?

All CAcafé blends contain milk and coconut. They are non-GMO and gluten-free, with no artificial colors or flavors.
