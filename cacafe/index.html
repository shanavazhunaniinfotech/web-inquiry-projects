Our story

From a doctor's kitchen to your morning.

Our founder, Dr. Ted Chao, trained in medicine and specialized in liver health. When his family moved to the U.S., he started blending herbal drinks in his Southern California kitchen that tasted as good as they made you feel.

The best daily habits are the ones you look forward to.

The idea CAcafé was built on
  1. Early ’90s

    Medicine first

    Dr. Chao graduates from medical school in China, specializing in liver health.

  2. 1999

    A new home

    The family moves to the U.S. and the first herbal blends take shape at home.

  3. 2004

    Into stores

    An earlier family venture brings kombucha to U.S. shelves.

  4. 2012

    CAcafé is born

    After a decade of prototyping, the first coconut blends launch.

  5. Today

    Nationwide

    On shelves at Costco, Walmart, Whole Foods & H-E-B, and delivered free from us.

    How do I prepare it?

    Add one to two tablespoons to 8 oz of hot or iced water or plant milk and stir. It dissolves in seconds, no frother needed. It also works in smoothies.

    How much caffeine is in a cup?

    Our Weight Management Coffee has about 45–50mg per serving, roughly half a standard brewed coffee, for a steady lift without the jitters.

    Is CAcafé dairy-free or vegan?

    All CAcafé blends contain milk and coconut. They're non-GMO and gluten-free, with no artificial colors or flavors.

    What if I don't love it?

    Every order is covered by our 30-day Love-It Guarantee. If a blend isn't your perfect cup, we'll exchange it or refund your purchase.
