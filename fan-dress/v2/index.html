What is a Fan Dress?

A Fan Dress reimagines the traditional jersey as an elevated, feminine dress: a licensed jersey-style top with a patented drawstring waist (women) or a built-in twirl skirt (girls & baby).

Are your products officially licensed?

Yes. All of our products are officially licensed.

Do the dresses run big or small?

Our NCAA dresses run slightly small. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for the best fit.

When will my order arrive?

Please allow 5–7 business days for your order to arrive. Orders over $100 ship free.

How do I wash my dress?

Machine wash cold (or hand wash in cold water) and lay flat to dry to maintain its shape and quality.

When will sold-out items be restocked?

We restock frequently. Join the Fan Club to get restock alerts first.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns on unworn items within 30 days of purchase. Start a return or email support@fandress.com.
