Our story / 09

“I wanted an elevated jersey dress for my daughter and myself, so I designed it, patented it, and turned it into a brand for female fans everywhere.”

Allison Brockman · Founder · Former Texas A&M Diamond Darling

  1. The spark

    Cutting up old A&M baseball jerseys in college to wear as a Diamond Darling.

  2. The search

    No jersey with a skirt existed for her daughter. A patent lawyer confirmed nobody had made one.

  3. The brand

    Patented, licensed and worn in the stands from Baton Rouge to Savannah.

As featured in Houston Chronicle

01What is a Fan Dress?

A Fan Dress reimagines the traditional jersey as an elevated, feminine dress: a licensed jersey-style top with a patented drawstring waist (women) or a built-in twirl skirt (girls & baby).

02Are your products officially licensed?

Yes. All of our products are officially licensed.

03Do the dresses run big or small?

Our NCAA dresses run slightly small. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for the best fit.

04When will my order arrive?

Please allow 5–7 business days for your order to arrive. Orders over $100 ship free.

05How do I wash my dress?

Machine wash cold (or hand wash in cold water) and lay flat to dry to maintain its shape and quality.

06When will sold-out items be restocked?

We restock frequently. Join the Fan Club above to get restock alerts first.

07What is your return policy?

We accept returns on unworn items within 30 days of purchase. Start a return or email support@fandress.com.
