Our story

“I wanted an elevated jersey dress for my daughter and myself, so I designed it, patented it, and turned it into a brand for female fans everywhere.”

Allison Brockman · Founder, Texas A&M Diamond Darling

After years of buying jerseys for her boys, Allison couldn't ignore what was missing for women and girls. She met her husband at Texas A&M, where he played baseball, and they got engaged on the field. That love for the game became Fan Dress.

What is a Fan Dress?

A Fan Dress reimagines the traditional jersey as an elevated, feminine dress: a licensed jersey-style top paired with a drawstring waist (women) or a built-in twirl skirt (girls & baby). It's patented and designed specifically for female fans.

Are your products officially licensed?

Yes. All of our products are officially licensed.

Do the dresses run big or small?

Our NCAA dresses run slightly small. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for the best fit.

When will my order arrive?

Please allow 5–7 business days for your order to arrive. Orders over $100 ship free.

How do I wash my dress?

Machine wash cold (or hand wash in cold water) and lay flat to dry to maintain its shape and quality.

When will sold-out items be restocked?

We restock frequently. Join the Fan Club list above to get restock alerts first.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns on unworn items within 30 days of purchase. Start your return through our returns portal or email support@fandress.com.
