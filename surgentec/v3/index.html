03Product launch

BCOR™Bone Marrow Aspirate Kit

Aspirate quality starts at the needle

01

CELLect Filtration Technology™

Twelve total 200-micron slits provide 2.5x more surface area for marrow extraction than competitive needles1, designed to support reduced intake velocity, maintain marrow integrity, and filter bone spicules at the point of collection.

An inner and outer cannula provide double 200-micron filtration to further reduce aspirate contaminants. Two passes. Greater consistency.1

02

Direct Syringe Connection

Direct syringe attachment eliminates stylet exchanges required in conventional workflows. Fewer steps. Reduced connection points.1

03

Bone Biopsy Capable

One entry. Two samples. After aspiration, use the included drill and plunger to collect a cancellous bone core biopsy from the same access site.1

04

Needle Relocation Sleeve

Enables precise mechanical repositioning of the needle tip, supporting the recommended 2 mL draw protocol for each position and designed to minimize peripheral blood ingress across the full aspiration sequence.1

1 Data on file. Device specifications, aspirate surface area comparison, and filtration performance based on in vitro testing and engineering analysis; may not be representative of clinical performance.

All components are provided to the hospital sterilized and single use only. Refer to product labeling for complete indications, contraindications, warnings, and precautions.

Note: Regulatory submissions reference this device as the ALARA BMA Neuro Access Kit (510(k) K243945).

07About SurGenTec®

SurGenTec® is a privately owned medical device company, developing, and manufacturing innovative products with patient and surgeon safety at the heart of everything we do.

Our product development team is dedicated to creating intuitive solutions to improve the quality of life for patients. By collaborating with experienced surgeons, leading experts, and global distributors we are able to continuously improve the design and engineering of our products.

Our patented technologies deliver safe, cost effective and unique solutions which benefit healthcare providers as well as their patients. Pursuant to 35 U.S.C. § 287(a), SurGenTec’s products are covered by or for use under one or more of our filed U.S. Patents.

We believe in service to our patients, surgeons, and team by focusing on a family-style culture empowering everyone to perform their best within our community.
