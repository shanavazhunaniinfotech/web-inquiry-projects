Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first conversation is free and without obligation. It's simply a chance to understand your needs and see if we're the right fit.

Which areas do you serve for real estate?

Residential clients throughout Massachusetts and Florida, including families relocating between the two states.

Do you interpret at immigration appointments?

Yes, Haitian Creole–English interpretation is available for certain immigration-related appointments, as well as medical, legal, school and personal meetings. Ask us about your specific appointment.

How far in advance should I book?

For interpretation, as early as possible. Tell us your date and we'll do our best even on short notice. For events, 3–12 months ahead is ideal.

How does payment work?

There's nothing to pay online. Pricing and payment are arranged personally with you once your service is confirmed.
