    How do I pay?

    Pay on delivery by M-Pesa is available for Nairobi deliveries. Store pick-up and upcountry orders are prepaid by M-Pesa or card. Our riders never collect cash.

    What if it doesn't fit?

    Contact us within 24 hours of delivery and swap for another item, or take a credit note valid for 90 days. Items must be unworn and unwashed, tags attached, in original packaging. We don't give cash refunds.

    What if something arrives damaged or wrong?

    Check your parcel on arrival and report it within 24 hours with photos, on WhatsApp or by email. If confirmed, we collect it free and send a replacement or another item of your choice.

    Do you have express delivery?

    Yes: priority delivery within 2 hours in inner Nairobi (8 am–4 pm) from KSh 800, same-evening delivery for orders placed 1–5 pm, scheduled delivery from KSh 600, and next-day upcountry for KSh 1,000. If we miss the promised express time, the express fee is waived.
