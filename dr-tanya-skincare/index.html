    Questions before you try?

    30-day skin promiseTry it for 30 days. If your skin doesn’t love it, we’ll make it right.
    Which products are right for my skin?

    Start with the 60-second skin quiz — it recommends a 3-step AM/PM routine for your concern. Or shop by concern above: acne, pigmentation, sensitive or dry skin.

    Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

    Our formulas are selected by Dr Tanya with sensitive skin in mind. Start slowly with actives like Retinol (2–3 nights a week) and always patch test first.

    When will I see results?

    Hydration is often felt straight away. Brightness and tone usually improve over 4–8 weeks of consistent use — skin renews roughly every 28 days.

    Can I use Vitamin C and Retinol together?

    Yes — just at different times. Super C Serum in the morning (with SPF), Advanced Retinol Serum at night. That’s exactly how the Skin Doctor Protocol is set up.
