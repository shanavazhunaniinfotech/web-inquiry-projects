Questions

Before you book

Still wondering? Email hello@melissajeanmedium.com and I'll reply personally.

I've never had a reading. What actually happens?

We meet privately on Zoom. I'll explain how I work, then share what comes through. You only need to answer yes, no or "I'm not sure". There's time at the end for your questions. Most people say it felt far more like a warm conversation than they expected.

Can you guarantee a specific loved one will come through?

No ethical medium can guarantee who comes through. I'll always be honest about what I receive. If no connection forms in the first part of a mediumship session, we can shift to an intuitive reading or reschedule.

How do time zones work?

All available times are shown automatically in your own time zone when you book, and your confirmation and calendar invite match it.

What's your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

You can reschedule yourself from your confirmation email up to 24 hours before your session. Policy details are placeholder and will match Melissa's Terms & Conditions.

Is my session private? Can I record it?

Every session is private and confidential. You're welcome to record it for yourself; just let me know at the start.

How do I pay?

Full payment is taken securely by card when you book. Prepaid mentorship packages and gift cards can be redeemed at checkout.

Do you work with clients outside the U.S.?

Yes. Sessions are online, so you can join from anywhere with a stable internet connection.
