[ 07 — FAQ ]

Questions before you get in touch?

Can't find your answer? Ask an advisor — it's free.

Ask an advisor
Is the initial assessment really free?

Yes. The first 15-minute call is free and without obligation. If your matter needs more work, you get a written fixed-fee quote — you only pay if you accept it.

How can you offer fixed fees?

For advisory work, German law allows fees to be agreed individually. Because we specialise in recurring business matters, we know the effort involved and can quote up front. If the scope changes, we tell you before any extra cost arises.

Is my information confidential?

Absolutely. Everything you share is protected by professional secrecy. Our systems are encrypted, hosted in Germany and fully GDPR-compliant.

Do you advise in English?

Yes — every advisor works in German and English, and documents can be drafted bilingually. Ideal for international founders and companies entering Germany.

Do I need to come to your office?

No. Most clients work with us entirely by phone, video and e-signature. You are of course welcome at our Berlin office.

What if my matter is urgent?

Choose “This week” in the case check or call us directly. Matters with deadlines are assessed the same working day.
