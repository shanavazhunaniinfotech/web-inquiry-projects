Everything you need to know

Do I need any medical training to use the kit?

No. The N9+ app guides you through each exam step by step with pictures and simple instructions. Most parents are comfortable after their first exam.

What ages and how many children does it cover?

One kit covers up to 5 children in your household, ages 1 to 21. Each child gets their own profile in the app. You pay one membership no matter how many children you have.

Does this replace my child's regular pediatrician?

Think of us as the fast, at-home option for everyday illnesses like fevers, coughs, ear pain, sore throats and rashes. If your physician thinks your child needs to be seen in person, they'll tell you during the visit.

Do you take insurance?

Anywhere Pediatrics is a self-pay service with transparent pricing: $99/month for the kit and $99 per visit. Many families use HSA or FSA funds, so check with your plan administrator.

Can we use it while traveling?

Yes. The kit fits in a carry-on, and your physician can send a prescription to a pharmacy near where you're staying.

Do I own the kit?

Your membership includes a 12-month lease of the complete N9+ Care Kit. We'll contact you about renewal options before your 12 months are up.

Is this for emergencies?

No. If your child has trouble breathing, is unresponsive, or you think it's an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away.
