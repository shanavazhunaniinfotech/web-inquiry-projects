FAQ

Good questions to ask first.

Can I buy property in Dubai as a foreigner?

Yes. Foreign nationals can buy freehold property in designated areas such as Palm Jumeirah, Downtown, Dubai Marina and Dubai Hills.

How safe is buying off-plan?

Off-plan projects must be registered with RERA, and buyer payments go into a project escrow account. We also check the developer's delivery record with you.

What costs are there on top of the price?

The main one is the 4% Dubai Land Department registration fee, plus smaller admin and Oqood registration fees. We list them all before you reserve.

Can I buy remotely from overseas?

Yes. Reservation, contracts and payments can be done remotely, and we handle inspections and handover for you.

Do you help with mortgages and the Golden Visa?

Yes. We connect you with mortgage advisors and guide the Golden Visa process for qualifying purchases of AED 2 million or more.
