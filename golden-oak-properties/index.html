FAQ

Good questions to ask first.

Can I buy property in Dubai as a foreigner?

Yes. Foreign nationals can buy freehold property in designated areas such as Palm Jumeirah, Downtown, Dubai Marina, Dubai Hills and Creek Beach, without needing UAE residency.

How safe is buying off-plan?

Off-plan projects must be registered with RERA, and buyer payments go into a project escrow account. We also check the developer's track record and the SPA terms with you before you commit.

What costs are there on top of the price?

The main one is the 4% Dubai Land Department registration fee, plus smaller admin and Oqood registration fees. We give you the full breakdown for each project.

Can I buy remotely from overseas?

Yes. Reservation, contracts and payments can be done remotely, and we handle inspections and handover for you.

Do you help with mortgages and the Golden Visa?

Yes. We connect you with mortgage advisors and guide the Golden Visa process for qualifying purchases of AED 2M and above.

Design & CRO notes

  1. Scroll-built hero. The empty plot turns into a finished tower and villa as you scroll, which is how off-plan buying works. The HUD shows the payment plan progressing with it, so the story also explains the product.
  2. Two CTAs at the start and end of the build. "Explore projects" for browsers, "Free consultation" for ready buyers. A "Skip the build" link keeps returning visitors fast.
  3. Developer marquee + stats right after the hero for instant trust.
  4. Search + filters + AED/USD/EUR switch for overseas buyers. Cards open a brochure request (lead capture) instead of a dead-end page.
  5. Payment plan estimator answers "how much do I need now?" and flags Golden Visa eligibility at AED 2M+.
  6. Areas list with hover preview supports SEO landing pages per community.
  7. Real Google reviews + owner presence build trust for a relationship-led business.
  8. 3-step consultation wizard qualifies leads (goal, budget) before asking for contact details, which raises completion.
  9. Mobile sticky bar (Call, WhatsApp, Consultation) because most Dubai property leads come from mobile and WhatsApp.
