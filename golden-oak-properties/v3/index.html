Investment advisory

Advice first. Inventory second.

Golden Oak was founded by a portfolio and risk-management specialist. Every investment conversation starts with your goals and your tolerance for risk, not with a sales list.

Brief
  1. 01

    Your brief & risk profile

    Goals, time horizon, budget and how much risk you're comfortable with: home, income, capital growth or a mix.

  2. 02

    Market & developer diligence

    Community, supply pipeline and the developer's delivery record, reviewed before any project reaches your shortlist.

  3. 03

    Structure & payment planning

    Off-plan or ready, single unit or portfolio. Official payment plans, fees and escrow explained line by line.

  4. 04

    Acquisition & handover

    Reservation, SPA, DLD registration, snagging and handover, managed with you from Business Bay.

  5. 05

    Portfolio review

    Leasing, resale timing and next acquisitions, reviewed as your portfolio grows.

Escrow

Off-plan payments go into a project escrow account regulated by RERA.

4% DLD

Dubai Land Department registration fee, shown upfront in every plan we share.

AED 2M+

Property purchases from AED 2 million may support a 10-year Golden Visa application, subject to government criteria.

Golden Oak does not promise returns, rental yields or capital appreciation. Past performance of any property or area is not a guide to the future.
