Furry FAQs

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Ask our assistant or reach us at 888 433 9089 · help@skooncatlitter.com

Is Skoon healthy and safe for my cat?

Skoon is made from diatomite, compressed deposits of fossilized algae skeletons — not clay strip-mined from the earth or synthetic chemical additives. It is 100% natural and non-toxic from the start, meets or exceeds OECD Test Guidelines, and is gentle enough for sensitive cats, kittens and multi-pet households.

How do I use Skoon Cat Litter?

Pour it into a clean litter box. Skoon does not clump: the pebbles absorb and dry, so the only thing you scoop is the occasional solid. When the pebbles turn off-white, dump and replace the whole batch.

How much litter do I need?

One 8 lb bag lasts 1 cat up to 1 month. Most homes subscribe to one bag per cat, delivered every 4 weeks.

Does Skoon work with automatic cat litter boxes?

Skoon Fine Grain is compatible with self-cleaning litter boxes. It has the same qualities as our original pebbles with a finer texture.

Subscribers get 10% off forever and free shipping forever. Skip, change your delivery schedule or cancel anytime from your account.

What if my cat doesn't like it?
