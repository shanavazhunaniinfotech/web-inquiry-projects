Why we exist

In many communities across Angola, women reach adulthood never having held a pen. Not for lack of brilliance — because education was never made accessible to them. We exist to change that world, one woman at a time.

Breaking the frame

She was told where her limits were. She stepped past them.

Spelling with Ella · Luanda · 2025

She ran her own pharmacy. Served in the armed forces. Raised ten children. And could not write her own name.

“Today I can read. I can speak up.”
— Linda, graduate
Project Mwana · Tailoring · Luanda · 2025

She arrived to learn tailoring. She graduated wearing a dress she had cut, pinned and stitched with her own hands.

“Muito obrigada pela oportunidade, Ella Africa.”
— Catarina, graduate (“Thank you so much for the opportunity”)
Spelling with Ella · Luanda · 2025

She grew up unable to read and stopped going to school. When she heard about free literacy classes, she came. Three months later, she graduated.

“Learning has no age limit. Education is a wonderful thing.”
— Isabel V. Romeu, graduate
