Good questions

Before you buy

Still unsure? Ask Jon on WhatsApp. Real answers, fast.

How do I know the watch is authentic?

Every watch is inspected before it's listed: serial and reference checked, movement opened where appropriate, and all parts verified as genuine. You can inspect it yourself in person, or take it to any authorized dealer. If it's ever proven not genuine, you get a full refund.

Can I see the watch in person before buying?

Yes, and we encourage it. Meet Jon at a safe, public location in the Cleveland area (or a local bank for larger purchases), try it on, and only pay when you're happy.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank wire, cashier's check, cash for in-person deals, and Zelle for smaller amounts. Ask about holding a watch with a deposit.

Do you ship? Is it insured?

Yes. We ship nationwide, fully insured for the full value, discreetly packed, with signature required on delivery. Tracking is shared as soon as it ships.

Do you take trade-ins?

Absolutely. Most of our clients trade up. Send a few photos through the sell/trade form and get an offer within 24 hours.

Is the price negotiable?

Prices are set fairly against the current market, but reasonable offers are always heard. Use “Make an offer” when you inquire about a watch.
