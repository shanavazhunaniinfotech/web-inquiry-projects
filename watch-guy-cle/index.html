Good questions

Before you buy.

Still unsure? Ask Jon on WhatsApp →

How do I know the watch is authentic?

Every watch is inspected before it's listed: serial and reference checked, movement inspected where appropriate, and all parts verified genuine. You're welcome to inspect it in person or have it checked by any authorized dealer. If it's ever proven not genuine, you get a full refund.

Can I see the watch before I pay?

Yes, and we encourage it. Meet Jon at a safe public location in the Cleveland area (or at your bank for larger purchases), try it on, and only pay when you're happy.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank wire, cashier's check, cash for in-person deals, and Zelle for smaller amounts. Ask about holding a watch with a deposit.

Do you ship, and is it insured?

Nationwide, insured for full value, discreetly packed, signature required on delivery. Tracking is shared the moment it ships.

Do you take trade-ins?

Absolutely. Most clients trade up. Use the sell / trade form for an offer within 24 hours.

Is the price negotiable?

Prices are set fairly against the current market, but reasonable offers are always heard. Choose “Make an offer” when you inquire.
