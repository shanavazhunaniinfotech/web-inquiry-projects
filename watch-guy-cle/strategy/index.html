Design rationale · Prepared for Jon Ibrahim

A one-page site built to turn browsers into buyers.

Every section has one job: remove a reason not to reach out. Below is why the page is ordered the way it is, how it maps to your Wix account, and how you'll update inventory yourself.

Tip: on the page, tap Design notes (bottom corner) to see these notes on top of each section.

The thinking

Three questions every watch buyer asks

Buying a $5k–$60k watch from an independent dealer comes down to trust. The design answers these three questions before the visitor has to ask them.

“Is it real?”

Authenticity guarantee in the top bar, hero, “Meet Jon” and FAQ, plus free photos and video of the serial on request.

“Is the price fair?”

Every price is shown openly (no “call for price”), with year, box and papers and condition next to it. “Make an offer” is a built-in option.

“Is it safe to buy?”

Meet in person in Cleveland or receive it fully insured with signature. A real person, a real face, real reviews.

Section order

Why each section sits where it does

  1. Cinematic hero, two clear doors

    A full-screen watch photo sells the feeling of ownership. The headline promises the outcome (the right watch, authentic, handed over in person). Explore the collection for browsers, Chat on WhatsApp for ready buyers. A glass “Just in” card puts a real, priced watch above the fold, and proof stats sit under the buttons.

  2. Trust bar + brand ticker

    Authenticity, meet-or-ship and a live Cleveland clock with “replies within the hour” in the header. A slow brand ticker answers “does he have Rolex?” instantly.

  3. Watch of the week (spotlight)

    One watch presented like a boutique window, with an animated ring, full specs and price. It gives undecided visitors an easy start, and the two highest-intent buttons on the page (Reserve this watch, Request live video) sit right beside the price.

  4. The collection

    Brand chips, price filter and sort that stay visible while scrolling. Every card shows year, box and papers, condition and price, with a ♥ to save, a one-tap WhatsApp that names the watch, and Quick view to see details without leaving the page. Sold pieces stay as proof and become “Find me one.”

  5. Watch Finder quiz

    Three taps (style, budget, who it's for) recommend real watches from the inventory, then capture a phone or email to “send me matches.” If nothing fits, it becomes a sourcing request. No visitor hits a dead end.

  6. Meet the Watch Guy

    A face, a first-person story and a signature, because at this price people buy from a person. Four promises each remove one fear: fakes, overpaying, scams, shipping risk.

  7. How it works

    Four steps along an animated gold line. It makes buying from an independent dealer feel short and safe, then offers a button at the moment of confidence.

  8. Sell or trade

    A second revenue stream and a sales tool (“trade your Tudor toward the Rolex”). Sellers become future inventory. Optional.

  9. Client stories + Instagram

    Reviews in clients' own words, then an Instagram strip that proves an active business and grows the following where repeat buyers watch for drops.

  10. FAQ

    Every objection (authenticity, viewing, payment, shipping, trade-ins, negotiation) answered openly.

  11. Contact: every channel

    WhatsApp, form (to Jon's email), Instagram, Facebook, email. The form's watch dropdown fills from inventory automatically.

  12. Footer: private new-arrivals list

    Captures visitors who aren't ready today, so Jon owns his buyer list instead of relying only on Instagram's algorithm.

Built in your Wix account

You own everything

  • Built directly in your Wix account. You keep full ownership of the site, domain and content.
  • Inventory runs on a Wix CMS collection connected to the watch grid (a Wix repeater).
  • Forms use Wix Forms, so every inquiry lands in your email (and in the Wix inbox / app).
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook buttons link to your real accounts.
  • Mobile layout is tuned separately in the Wix mobile editor.

Updating inventory: 1-minute guide

From the Wix dashboard or the Wix app on your phone → CMS → Inventory:

  1. Add a watch: “Add item”, fill in brand, model, reference, year, price, box & papers, condition, then upload photos.
  2. Edit: click any field (price, description, photos) and change it. It's live right away.
  3. Mark as sold: switch Status to “Sold”. The card greys out and becomes “Find me one like this.”
  4. Remove: delete the item, or hide it to keep it for your records.

No code, no developer. A short PDF and video walkthrough come with the handover.
