The thinking

Three questions every watch buyer asks

Buying a $5k–$60k watch from an independent dealer comes down to trust. The design answers these three questions before the visitor has to ask them.

“Is it real?”

Authenticity guarantee in the top bar, hero, “Meet Jon” and FAQ, plus free photos and video of the serial on request.

“Is the price fair?”

Every price is shown openly (no “call for price”), with year, box and papers and condition next to it. “Make an offer” is a built-in option.

“Is it safe to buy?”

Meet in person in Cleveland or receive it fully insured with signature. A real person, a real face, real reviews.

Section order

Why each section sits where it does

  1. Trust bar

    Authenticity, meet or insured shipping, and “usually replies within 1 hour” with live Cleveland time. These cover the three biggest worries in the first second.

  2. Hero with two CTAs

    The headline sells the outcome (an authentic watch from someone you trust). Shop watches for browsers, WhatsApp Jon for ready buyers. A “Just in” card shows real stock above the fold. Proof numbers sit under the buttons.

  3. Brand strip

    Visitors scan for “does he have Rolex?” This answers it instantly and lends brand prestige.

  4. Inventory right after the hero

    Most visitors come to see what's for sale, so we don't make them scroll through an “About” first. Brand and price filters, key facts on every card, and two actions per watch: Inquire (pre-filled form) and WhatsApp (pre-written message naming the watch). Sold watches stay as proof and become “Find me one like this.”

  5. “Can't find it?” sourcing request

    With only 10 pieces, most visitors won't see their exact watch. This turns those visitors into leads with a budget instead of lost traffic.

  6. Meet the Watch Guy + 4 guarantees

    People buy from people. A face, a short story and a signature, then four guarantees that each remove one specific fear.

  7. How it works (4 steps)

    Shows exactly what happens after you inquire, which makes buying from an independent dealer feel simple and safe.

  8. Sell or trade

    A second revenue stream and a powerful sales tool (“trade your Tudor toward the Rolex”). Sellers become future inventory. Optional: we can remove it if you only sell.

  9. Reviews + Instagram

    Proof in clients' own words, then your Instagram feed to show an active, real business and grow your followers.

  10. FAQ

    Each question is an objection: authenticity, viewing in person, payment, shipping, trade-ins, negotiation. Answered openly, so there's no reason left not to reach out.

  11. Contact: every channel

    WhatsApp (fastest), the inquiry form (straight to your email), Instagram, Facebook and email. The form's watch dropdown is filled from your inventory automatically.

  12. Footer: new-arrival alerts

    Captures visitors who aren't ready today, so you build your own buyer list instead of relying only on the Instagram algorithm.

Built in your Wix account

You own everything

  • Built directly in your Wix account. You keep full ownership of the site, domain and content.
  • Inventory runs on a Wix CMS collection connected to the watch grid (a Wix repeater).
  • Forms use Wix Forms, so every inquiry lands in your email (and in the Wix inbox / app).
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook buttons link to your real accounts.
  • Mobile layout is tuned separately in the Wix mobile editor.

Updating inventory: 1-minute guide

From the Wix dashboard or the Wix app on your phone → CMS → Inventory:

  1. Add a watch: “Add item”, fill in brand, model, reference, year, price, box & papers, condition, then upload photos.
  2. Edit: click any field (price, description, photos) and change it. It's live right away.
  3. Mark as sold: switch Status to “Sold”. The card greys out and becomes “Find me one like this.”
  4. Remove: delete the item, or hide it to keep it for your records.

No code, no developer. A short PDF and video walkthrough come with the handover.
