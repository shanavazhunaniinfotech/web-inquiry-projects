The two directions

Same story, two personalities.

Version A

Best if: Jon wants to feel established, trusted and classic, the way a high-end jeweller does.

The thinking

Three questions every watch buyer asks

Buying a $5k–$60k watch from an independent dealer is a trust decision. Every section answers one of these before the visitor has to ask.

“Is it real?”

Authenticity guarantee in the top bar, hero, spotlight, Meet Jon and FAQ, plus live video of the serial on request.

“Is the price fair?”

Every price shown openly, with year, box and papers and condition beside it. “Make an offer” is built into every inquiry.

“Is it safe to buy?”

Meet in person in Cleveland or fully insured shipping. A real face, real reviews, a reply within the hour.

Built in your Wix account

You own everything

  • Built directly in your Wix account. You keep full ownership of the site, domain and content.
  • Inventory runs on a Wix CMS collection connected to the watch grid.
  • Forms use Wix Forms, so every inquiry lands in your email (and the Wix app).
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook buttons link to your real accounts.
  • Mobile layout tuned separately in the Wix mobile editor.

Updating inventory: 1-minute guide

From the Wix dashboard or the Wix app on your phone → CMS → Inventory:

  1. Add a watch: “Add item”, fill in brand, model, reference, year, price, box & papers, condition, then upload photos.
  2. Edit: change any field. It's live right away.
  3. Mark as sold: switch Status to “Sold”. The card turns into “Find me one like it.”
  4. Feature it: tick “Watch of the week”.
  5. Remove: delete the item, or hide it to keep your records.
