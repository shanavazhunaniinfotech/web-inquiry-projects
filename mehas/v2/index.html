How long does each fragrance last?

MEHAS fragrances are concentrated Eaux de Parfum. Expect 8–12 hours on skin depending on the fragrance; on fabric, the trail often lasts into the next day. Monarch lasts longest.

How fast is delivery, and is it free?

Express delivery is free across the UAE. Order before 6pm for next-day delivery in Dubai and Abu Dhabi; 2–3 working days to other Emirates.

I can’t smell it first. What if it’s not for me?

Start with the Discovery Set (3 × 10 mL, AED 95) and use the full AED 95 as credit towards a full bottle. Unopened full bottles can be returned within 14 days.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Tabby and Tamara (4 interest-free payments), and cash on delivery.

Are MEHAS perfumes authentic?

Every bottle is sold directly by MEHAS Perfumes UAE and arrives sealed in its original presentation box.
