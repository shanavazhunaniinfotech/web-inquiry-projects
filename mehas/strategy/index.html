Redesign proposal · Home page & product page

Why every section is where it is.

The brief: give MEHAS the calm, editorial luxury of Aesop — clean layouts, refined typography, product-first presentation — while making the store convert. Below is the reasoning behind each decision.

View home pageView product page

What we took from Aesop

  • Restraint: a warm, muted palette (cream, stone, ink), with colour coming only from the products. MEHAS’s own photography already sits on a warm stone backdrop, so the site is tuned to it and every bottle looks like it belongs.
  • Typography as the luxury signal: a classical serif that echoes the MEHAS wordmark, paired with a clean sans for UI. No gimmicks, lots of whitespace.
  • Product detail as a “spec sheet”: Aesop’s signature PDP list (scent family, key notes, wear it for, season, size), adapted for fragrance.
  • Editorial pacing: large imagery, alternating light and dark bands, one idea per section.
Why it’s Aesop-inspired, not an Aesop copy: a pixel-for-pixel clone would mean reusing another brand’s design, copy and identity. That’s a legal (trade-dress/copyright) risk, and it would make MEHAS look like an imitation. Instead we follow the same design principles, built around MEHAS’s own bottles, colours and story. The look is just as premium, and it belongs to MEHAS.

Product page — what makes it convert

  • Buy box in decision order: name and character, then rating, story, spec sheet, format, add to bag, then reassurance.
  • Format selector: 100 mL, the Trilogy (best value) or the Discovery Set. Each visitor sees an option that fits their budget and confidence.
  • Live delivery countdown (“Order within 3h 12m for delivery tomorrow”) adds real urgency without fake scarcity.
  • Pay in 4 (Tabby/Tamara) shown under the price. An AED 200 bottle reads as AED 50.
  • Gallery: bottle, box, mood image and composition card. On mobile it becomes a swipe gallery.
  • Character + composition sections answer “how long does it last, how strong is it, how does it smell over time”.
  • Sticky add-to-bag bar appears once the main button scrolls away, so there’s always one tap to buy.
  • Cart drawer with a progress bar towards a free 10 mL travel spray, plus “complete your wardrobe” cross-sells.

Built to scale on Shopify 2.0

Every section above becomes a reusable Shopify 2.0 section with blocks (hero tiles, USP items, reviews, FAQ items, ritual steps). New fragrances get their notes, meters and “wear it for” data from product metafields, so adding product #4 or #40 needs no code. The product page is one template that works for every fragrance.
