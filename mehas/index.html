The ritual

Make it last from morning to majlis.

Prepare the skin

Fragrance holds longest on moisturised skin. Apply after a shower, on an unscented lotion or a touch of oil.

Pulse points, not wrists

Two sprays on the neck and one on the chest. Don’t rub — it breaks the top notes before they open.

Layer on fabric

A light mist on your kandura, abaya or jacket carries the trail through the evening and into the next morning.

How long does each fragrance last?

MEHAS fragrances are concentrated Eaux de Parfum. On skin, expect 8–12 hours depending on the fragrance and your skin; on fabric, the trail often lasts into the next day. Monarch is our longest-lasting.

How fast is delivery, and is it free?

Express delivery is free across the UAE. Order before 6pm for next-day delivery in Dubai and Abu Dhabi; 2–3 working days to the other Emirates.

I can’t smell it first. What if it’s not for me?

Start with the Discovery Set (3 × 10 mL, AED 95). Wear each for a week, then use the full AED 95 as credit towards your full bottle. Unopened full bottles can be returned within 14 days.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Tabby and Tamara (4 interest-free payments), and cash on delivery.

Are MEHAS perfumes authentic?

Every bottle is sold directly by MEHAS Perfumes UAE — never through grey-market resellers — and arrives sealed in its original presentation box.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Add a complimentary gift wrap and handwritten note at checkout, and ship directly to the recipient. Prices are never included in the parcel.
