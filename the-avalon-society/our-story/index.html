Our Story

We didn’t want to choose between beautiful and protected.

The Avalon Society began at home, with a simple frustration and a better idea.

Our purpose

It all started at home. As pet owners, we couldn’t find anything to protect our floors that we actually wanted to look at, and no one should have to sacrifice their style to keep their home safe from their pets.

So we made our own: a washable, leak-proof mat with the look of a fine rug. Without meaning to, we designed something versatile, functional and beautiful enough to stay, from the front door to the kitchen, the bathroom and the sofa.

Natalie Youn, Founder
