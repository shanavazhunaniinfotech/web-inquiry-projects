FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Message us. We reply within minutes during opening hours.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free, but booking guarantees your time with zero waiting. Weekday evenings and Saturdays fill up fast.

Do I have to pay when I book?

No. Booking is free and you pay after your service by M-Pesa, e-Mola, card or cash.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Just reply to your WhatsApp confirmation. Free changes up to 2 hours before your appointment.

What if I’m not happy with my cut?

Come back within 7 days and we’ll adjust it for free. No questions asked. That’s our “love it” guarantee.

Do you cut kids’ and textured or curly hair?

Yes to both. Kids under 12 get a dedicated Kids Cut, and Amina specialises in curls, coils and textured hair.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes. Free street parking on Av. Julius Nyerere and a secure car park 50 m from the shop.
