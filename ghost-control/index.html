08FAQ

Good to know.

Still have a question? We reply on WhatsApp within minutes during opening hours.

Ask on WhatsApp →
Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free, but booking guarantees your time with zero waiting. Weekday evenings and Saturdays fill up fast.

Do I pay when I book?

No. Booking is free. You pay after your service by M-Pesa, e-Mola, card or cash.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Reply to your WhatsApp confirmation. Changes are free up to 2 hours before your appointment.

What if I’m not happy with my cut?

Come back within 7 days and we’ll adjust it for free. No questions asked.

Do you cut kids’ and curly hair?

Yes. Kids under 12 get a dedicated Kids Cut, and Dário specialises in curls, coils and textured hair.

Is there parking?

Free street parking on Av. Julius Nyerere and a secure car park 50 m from the shop.
