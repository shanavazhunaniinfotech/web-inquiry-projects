Design direction

Premium, consistent and confident, like the world’s best barbershops.

Top barbershop brands share four traits: strong photography, bold type, a strict grid and restraint. We applied all four so Ghost Control looks established, trustworthy and worth the price.

One photographic style

Every image uses the same black-and-white grade, so the site feels like one cohesive shoot. Your real photos will be graded the same way, so it stays consistent as new work is added.

Bold, readable type

A strong modern sans-serif for headlines and a clean one for text. Large, high-contrast headings can be scanned in seconds on any phone.

A strict grid

Thin dividing lines and consistent spacing keep every section aligned. That structure reads as professional and makes prices and options easy to compare.

One accent colour, used with purpose

Brass gold appears only on things that matter: booking buttons, prices, ratings and key words. Your eye always lands on the next action.

The conversion journey

The page answers each question a visitor has, in order.

  1. 1. Is this for me?Hero: what you do, where, and the promise (precision, no waiting).
  2. 2. Can I trust them?4.9★ rating, numbers, promises, reviews and real barbers.
  3. 3. What does it cost?Full price list with clear prices and durations.
  4. 4. Are they good?Portfolio of real cuts and barber profiles.
  5. 5. How do I book?A booking button in every section, a 4-step booking flow and WhatsApp.

Section-by-section breakdown

Why each section is there, and why it sits where it does.

01

Hero: the first 5 seconds

What it does

  • The headline sells the two biggest reasons people switch barbers: a better cut (precision) and no waiting.
  • One primary gold button (“Book your chair”), plus “See prices” for people who want the cost first.
  • The live info bar answers the four instant questions: rating (trust), today’s hours (can I go now?), next free times (tap one to book) and location.
  • The header stays reachable, with live open/closed status, WhatsApp and Book always visible.

Why it converts

  • Visitors decide within seconds whether to stay. Clear value, social proof and a visible next step stop them bouncing. Showing real free times creates honest urgency and removes the “will they have space?” doubt.
Measures: bounce rate · booking starts

02

About: positioning and proof

  • One sentence of positioning, “run like a precision studio”, sets you apart from ordinary barbershops.
  • Four proof numbers (4.9 rating, 15,000+ cuts, 97% rebook, 9 years’ average experience) build credibility at a glance.
Measures: scroll depth

03

Services & prices, placed high

  • Not knowing the price is one of the top reasons people leave without booking. The full price list appears early, grouped into Haircuts, Beard & Shave and Packages.
  • Every service has its own Book button with that service already selected, which skips a step.
  • “Most booked” guides undecided visitors. “Save 100 MT” on the combo encourages a bigger booking.
  • The Signature Ritual stays on screen as you scroll the list. As the top-price option it makes the other prices feel reasonable and lifts the average spend.
  • “Not sure?” sends people to WhatsApp with a photo, so undecided visitors are captured instead of lost.
Measures: average booking value

04

Four promises: objection handling

  • Each promise answers a common fear about trying a new barbershop: waiting (on-time guarantee), hygiene, skill (master barbers) and a bad result (free fix within 7 days).
  • Guarantees reduce risk, which is especially important for first-time clients.
Measures: new-client conversion

05

Our work: proof of skill

  • A tight portfolio grid showing a range of styles and hair types. People choose barbers with their eyes.
  • “Book this look” appears at the moment of desire. The Instagram link adds more social proof.
  • Placeholder photos for now. We’ll replace them with your real cuts, graded in the same style.
Measures: clicks to book from gallery

06

The team: people book people

  • Faces build trust. Each barber shows a rating, speciality and years of experience.
  • “Book with [name]” opens the booking with that barber already selected, which loyal clients love. New clients can choose “any barber” for the fastest slot.
Measures: repeat bookings

07

Reviews: third-party trust

  • The Google rating badge is trusted more than anything the shop says about itself.
  • Each review answers a different doubt: punctuality, premium value, family-friendly, hygiene. We can connect your live Google reviews.
Measures: trust → booking starts

08

Offer & membership: a reason to act now

  • 15% off the first visit lowers the barrier for new clients. The code copies with one tap and is applied automatically in the booking flow.
  • Ghost Club turns one-off clients into monthly recurring revenue with priority booking.
  • On desktop, a one-time exit offer wins back visitors who are about to leave.
Measures: new clients · memberships

09

FAQ: clear the final doubts

  • Answers the questions that stop bookings (walk-ins, payment, cancelling, guarantee, kids, parking). Anything else goes to WhatsApp, so nobody leaves with an unanswered question.
Measures: drop-off before booking

10

Visit: built for local search

  • Today’s hours highlight automatically, with one-tap directions and calling.
  • The page includes LocalBusiness data for Google (rating, hours, address), which helps you appear in Maps and “barber near me” searches.
Measures: calls · direction requests

11

Final call to action

  • Visitors who scroll to the end are high-intent. A bold closing headline, the 3-step booking summary, both booking options and live “chairs left today” turn that intent into a booking.
Measures: bottom-of-page conversions

12

The booking flow: the conversion engine

  • Opens instantly from any Book button, with no page reload: service → barber → day & time → details.
  • Smart shortcuts: whatever the visitor already chose (a service, a barber or a time from the hero) is pre-filled and those steps are skipped.
  • A progress bar, crossed-out booked times and “only X slots left” keep people moving.
  • The request is sent to WhatsApp (no app or account needed), with an add-to-calendar option to cut no-shows.
  • It can later connect to a booking system (Fresha, Booksy, Square, etc.) for fully automatic scheduling.
Measures: booking completion rate

Mobile first

Most clients will find you on their phone.

  • Sticky booking bar: Call, WhatsApp and Book (with the next free time) stay within thumb reach after the hero.
  • Booking slides up from the bottom, like a native app, with large touch targets.
  • Price list, barbers and reviews reflow into easy single-column layouts. Nothing is hidden, nothing is cramped.
  • Lightweight code and optimised images for fast loading on mobile data.

Measurement & next steps

Built to measure, then improve.

Every key action (booking opened, each booking step, booking sent, WhatsApp click, call click, promo code copied) is already tracked and ready for Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel, so we can see what converts and keep improving it.
