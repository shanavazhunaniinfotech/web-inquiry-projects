About the property

A house with a story hung in every room

Built as a farmhouse in 1898 and lovingly restored by the family who still runs it, Willowmere is the kind of place you remember by its smells: fresh bread, woodsmoke and the lavender by the porch.

Since 1898

Four generations, one front porch

What began as a dairy farmhouse became a family home, then a guest house in 1979. Today Justin and family welcome travellers the way their grandparents did: by name, with a pot of coffee already on.

  • 1898Built
  • 4thGeneration
  • 12k+Happy guests
Five rooms, no two alike

Old bones, soft new linen

Original oak floors, deep window seats and a library lounge with an honesty bar. Every room has been restored with locally made furniture, organic linens and a proper reading lamp.

Three acres to wander

Willows, roses & a kitchen garden

A weeping willow shades the pond, a rose walk leads to the orchard, and the kitchen garden grows most of what you’ll eat at breakfast. Hammocks are first come, first served.

  • 60+Rose varieties

Breakfast, the main event

Three courses, no alarm clock

Served 8 to 10:30 in the sunroom or on the terrace: fresh-baked pastries, eggs from our hens, seasonal fruit and a hot dish that changes daily. Every dietary need gladly met.

Five minutes to the village, fifteen to three family wineries, and a river trail right from our gate. We’ll pack you a picnic and lend you the bikes.
