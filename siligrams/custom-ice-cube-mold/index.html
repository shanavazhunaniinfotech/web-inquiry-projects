What's the minimum order?

50 molds per design. Each design is priced on its own quantity.

How does the proof work?

Submit your configuration and artwork with no payment. Within 3 business days a designer sends your proof. Request changes as often as you like, then approve and pay in one step.

Standard vs. Rush?

Rush shortens production, not just shipping. Both show an estimated arrival date before you request.

Can I order more than one design?

Yes. Each design is its own line item on your proof, priced on its own quantity.

What is a spec sample?

A physical sample of your exact mold made before full production, for a small deposit credited to your final order.
