What's the minimum order?

50 molds per design. Each design is priced on its own quantity, and price per mold drops at 100, 250, 500 and 1,000+.

Do I pay before I see a proof?

No. Request your proof for free. You only pay after you've seen your logo on the proof and approved it. Changes are unlimited.

How long does it take?

Your proof arrives within 3 business days. After you approve, choose Standard or Rush production. We show estimated arrival dates before you request.

Can you match our brand color?

Yes. Choose Black, Navy or Red at no extra cost, or any custom Pantone for a one-time fee.

Is the silicone food safe?

Yes. 100% platinum silicone, free of lead, BPA and phthalates. Food-contact test results and an SDS are available on the product page.
