Good to know.

What's the minimum order?

50 molds per design. Price per mold drops at 100, 250, 500 and 1,000+.

Do I pay before I see a proof?

No. Your proof is free. You pay only after you've seen your logo on the proof and approved it. Revisions are unlimited.

How long does it take?

Proof within 3 business days. After approval choose Standard or Rush production. You'll see estimated arrival dates before you request.

Can you match our brand color?

Yes. Black, Navy and Red are included, or any custom Pantone for a one-time fee.

Is the silicone food safe?

Yes. 100% platinum silicone, free of lead, BPA and phthalates. Food-contact test results and an SDS are on the product page.
