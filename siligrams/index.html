FAQs

Questions, answered.

Talk to a real personMon–Fri, 7:30am–3:30pm ET978-451-6612sales@siligrams.com
What's the minimum order?

50 molds per design. Price per mold drops at 100, 250, 500 and 1,000+.

Do I pay before I see a proof?

No. Your proof is free. You only pay after you've seen your logo on the proof and approved it. Revisions are unlimited.

How long does it take?

Your proof arrives within 3 business days. After approval, choose Standard or Rush production. We show estimated arrival dates before you request.

Can you match our brand color?

Yes. Black, Navy and Red are included, or any custom Pantone for a one-time fee.

Is it food safe?

Yes. 100% platinum silicone, free of lead, BPA and phthalates. Test results and an SDS are on the product page.

What if I don't have a vector logo?

No problem. Upload any file, or tick "help me with my artwork". Our designers make it ice-ready for free.
