What makes Technogel® unlike anything you've slept on

01

It adapts in three dimensions

Foam only squashes down. Technogel® also moves sideways and in every direction, so it shapes itself to your head, neck and body in any position. There's no "sinking" feeling and no pushing back.

  • Fits back, side & stomach sleepers
  • Instant response when you turn
02

It draws heat away from your body

The gel has a very high heat capacity, and our patented tower design lets air flow through. Together they carry heat away from your body. Clinical studies show a gentle drop in body temperature, which helps you reach and stay in deep sleep longer.

  • Cool-to-touch, all night
  • Patented ventilation channels
03

It spreads pressure evenly

Shoulders and hips carry most of your weight. Technogel® spreads that pressure over a larger area, which relieves pressure points and lets your muscles relax. That's why hospitals and wheelchair users have relied on our gel since 1990.

  • Fewer pressure points
  • Permanently elastic, never flattens

FAQ

Questions before you sleep on it?

Can't find your answer? Email info@technogel.us. Our Pittsburgh team is happy to help.

What is Technogel®?

Technogel® is a unique polyurethane gel made without plasticizer oils. It was first developed and patented with Bayer and is now licensed exclusively to Technogel. Unlike foam, it deforms in three dimensions, so it adapts to your body and spreads pressure evenly.

Does the gel really keep me cooler?

Yes. The gel has a high heat capacity, so it absorbs body heat. Our patented tower design and ventilation holes let air flow through. Clinical studies have shown a gentle drop in body temperature, which helps you sleep more deeply.

Which pillow is right for me?

That depends on how you sleep. Back sleepers usually love the Lab Back & Side, side sleepers and hot sleepers the Original Deluxe, and anyone who wants maximum luxury the Vive. Take our 30-second quiz for a personal match.

Is Technogel® safe and hypoallergenic?

Our gel contains no harmful substances and is also used in medical products. It is odorless and hypoallergenic. Our pillows are certified by OEKO-TEX® and tested by the Hohenstein Institute.

What if it's not right for me?

Every pillow comes with a 100-night comfort trial. If you're not happy, contact us and we'll make it right.

How do I care for my pillow?

The outer cover is removable and washable. The antibacterial inner cover protects the gel and foam core, so your pillow stays fresh for years.
