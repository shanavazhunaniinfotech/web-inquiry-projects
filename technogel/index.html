01 · 3D support

Moves like water.
Supports like a solid.

Foam only gives way downward. Technogel® also moves up, down, left and right, so it spreads your weight evenly from head to toe. It relieves tension and keeps your spine aligned while you move freely.

  • Relieves more pressure than standard or memory foam
  • Fewer pressure points, less waking up with numbness

02 · Thermoregulation

Pulls heat away.
Keeps it away.

The gel has a naturally high thermal capacity, so it soaks up body heat and carries it off. Our patented tower-grid design keeps air moving. Foam is different: its tiny air chambers trap heat right against your skin.

  • Patented tower-grid airflow
  • Patented vertical ventilation in every pillow

03 · Clinically proven

Deeper sleep.
Measurably.

In clinical tests, sleeping on Technogel® lowered core body temperature and heart rate, which led to deeper, more continuous sleep. You wake up refreshed, not hot and restless.

  • Lower core body temperature
  • Lower heart rate · more continuous sleep

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Our Pittsburgh team is happy to help: info@technogel.us

Is Technogel® just gel-infused foam?

No. Gel-infused foam has particles mixed into the foam. Technogel® is a solid, patented layer of pure polyurethane gel, made without plasticizers, on top of premium memory foam. That's why it cools and supports consistently without heat building up.

Which pillow is right for me?

Back sleepers love the Anatomic. Side and back sleepers love the Contour, and the Side Sleeper has a shoulder cut-out. If you move around, choose the Deluxe. The Vive® Deluxe has an extra-thick gel layer for maximum luxury. Take the 60-second pillow finder for your personal match.

What if it doesn't suit me?

Every order is backed by a 30-night risk-free trial with free shipping and returns. Try it, love it, or return it.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. Technogel® is non-toxic, odorless and hypoallergenic. It is certified to OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (98.0.2425), which approves it for direct skin contact and even newborns. The covers have an antibacterial finish.

How do I care for my pillow?

The outer cover is removable and washable, and the antibacterial inner cover protects the core. Replacement covers are available too.

Where are Technogel® products made?

Our gel and products are made in our own facilities in Germany and Italy, combining German engineering with Italian craftsmanship.
