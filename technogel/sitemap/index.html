A proposed structure for the new global Technogel® website: main navigation, page hierarchy, multilingual URL strategy (EN · DE · IT) and a redirect plan. It is based on a full review of every page of the current site in all three languages.

01 · Summary

One clear structure for consumers and business partners

The current site already has strong content: 29 products, 9 B2B industries, technology, company and news. But it is hard to navigate and inconsistent between languages, and some of it can't be found by search engines. The proposed structure keeps all existing content and organises it under six main menu items plus a "Where to Buy" hub. Every language then has exactly the same pages.

67 · 59 · 62Current pages in EN · DE · IT. The language versions are not aligned.
91Proposed pages per language, the same in EN, DE and IT (273 in total)
6 + 1Main menu items, plus a "Where to Buy" utility hub
100%Existing content carried over, with 301 redirects from every old URL
  1. Split the two audiences clearly"Products" for consumers and "B2B Solutions" for industry, each with its own menu, content and inquiry path.
  2. Turn collections into real pagesOriginal, Vive®, Adjustable, Pixel, Lab, Travel, Advance and more get their own pages instead of anchors on one long page.
  3. Make "Technology" a hubDiscover the Gel becomes five focused pages: the gel, 3D support, cooling, sleep science, and quality & certifications.
  4. Same structure in every languageLanguage in the URL (/en/, /de/, /it/) with translated, readable slugs and hreflang. No more cookie redirects.
  5. One home for downloadsConsumer warranties & manuals in one place, and B2B data sheets & catalogs in another.
  6. A "Where to Buy" hubStore locator, distributors and official online shops together, always one click away.

02 · Audiences & key journeys

Who the site is for, and the shortest path for each

Every navigation decision below is designed to get each of these four groups to their goal in three clicks or fewer.

Consumers

Want better, cooler sleep and need to choose a pillow or mattress and find where to buy it.

Home → Products → Pillows → Original → Deluxe → Where to Buy

B2B buyers & engineers

Evaluate the gel for their own product: properties, Shore hardness, data sheets, process.

Home → B2B Solutions → Industries → Medical → Data sheet → Start a Project

Retailers & distributors

Need product information and partner contacts, and want to sell Technogel® in their market.

Utility bar → Where to Buy → Distributors → Contact

Press, talent & partners

Look for company facts, news, sustainability and career opportunities.

Company → About · Careers  |  News → Press & Media Kit

High

Language is stored in a cookie, not in the URL

Pages such as /company, /news and /discover show English, German or Italian depending on a cookie. Visitors and tools without that cookie (search engines, link previews, our audit tools) end up in an endless redirect loop. Search engines also can't tell the language versions apart.

New site: Every page gets its own language URL (/en/, /de/, /it/) plus hreflang tags. There are no cookie redirects.

High

Collections are only anchors, not pages

Original, Vive, Adjustable, Pixel, Lab, Travel, Advance and so on are sections of one long page (#original). They can't rank, be linked or be shared on their own.

New site: Each collection becomes its own page (13 new collection pages).

High

Content differs between languages

Careers (Jobs) exists only in German. Distributors and Accessories (ReCover) exist only in English. The Pixel collection is missing in Italian, and the German site has no mattress product pages. Several news articles are not translated.

New site: One shared structure for all three languages. Gaps are agreed and filled, or hidden on purpose.

Medium

Broken links & 404s

/en/B2B/overview/Sport%20Arms, /www.technogelworld.com/discover, /de_DE/produkte/matratzen-kollektion (the live page is '…matratzen-kollektion_copy') and /www.technogelworld.com/it_IT/acopri-il-gel all return 404.

New site: 301 redirect plan for all old URLs, plus a link check before launch.

Medium

URLs are not readable

Product and news URLs contain IDs and typos: viver-deluxe~p1867, news-12~n8249. The Italian 'Discover' slug is 'acopri-il-gel'.

New site: Short, readable, translated slugs (see URL strategy).

Medium

Downloads are scattered

More than 20 warranty PDFs sit under Legal, and the same 7 B2B data sheets are repeated on all 9 industry pages. Catalogs can only be found inside individual pages.

New site: A 'Care, Warranty & Manuals' page for consumers and a 'Downloads & Data Sheets' page for B2B.

Medium

No dedicated B2B lead path

B2B inquiries go through the same general contact form as consumer questions.

New site: A 'Start a Project' page and topic routing on the contact form.

Low

Labels & typos

Page titles 'Privavy Policy'; the Distributors page is titled 'Discover the Gel' and Accessories 'Living Accessories'; 'Sport Arms' vs 'Shooting sports'; menu typos 'Impluse', 'Recoild', 'Adhesions Tapes', 'accessorie'; Italian 'Impronta' for Imprint.

New site: One naming glossary for EN, DE and IT, with copy checked during the build.

Low

Where-to-buy is spread out

Store Locator sits in the top bar, the Distributors page exists only in English and is hard to find, and online shopping sits behind a separate Shop Online button.

New site: A single 'Where to Buy' hub: Store Locator, Distributors, Online Shops.

    07 · Languages & URL strategy

    Three languages, one structure, readable URLs

    Each language lives in its own folder with translated slugs. Pages are linked to their translations with hreflang so that search engines show German visitors the German page, and so on. Product names stay in English in all languages because they are brand names.

    PageEnglishDeutschItaliano

    Rules

    • Language in the URL, never in a cookie: /en/, /de/, /it/. Every page can be shared and indexed.
    • Same pages in every language: if a product isn't sold in a market, it is labelled "not available in your region" rather than missing.
    • The switcher keeps you on the same page: switching from /en/…/deluxe/ opens /de/…/deluxe/, not the home page.
    • First visit: the browser language is suggested in a small banner, with no automatic redirect.
    • hreflang + x-default on all pages, with a separate XML sitemap per language.
