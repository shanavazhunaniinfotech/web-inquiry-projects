[ 10 ] FAQ

Good to know.

Still unsure? Email info@technogel.us

Is Technogel® just gel-infused foam?

No. Gel-infused foam has gel particles mixed into the foam. Technogel® is a solid, patented layer of pure polyurethane gel, made without plasticizers, on top of premium memory foam.

Which pillow is right for me?

Back sleepers love the Anatomic, side and back sleepers the Contour, broad-shouldered side sleepers the Side Sleeper, and combination sleepers the Deluxe. Want maximum luxury? Choose the Vive®. Or take the 60-second finder.

What if it doesn't suit me?

Every order comes with a 30-night risk-free trial and free shipping & returns.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. Technogel® is non-toxic, odorless and hypoallergenic, and certified to OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 for direct skin contact, even for newborns.

How do I care for my pillow?

The outer cover is removable and washable. An antibacterial inner cover protects the core. Replacement covers are available.
