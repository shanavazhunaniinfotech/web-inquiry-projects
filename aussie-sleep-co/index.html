How is freight calculated?+

At checkout we send your postcode and each item's weight and size to our carrier for a live quote. If your order ships from two or three warehouses, each leg is quoted and the total is shown before you pay.

Can I pick up my order?+

Yes. Click & collect is free from our Sydney and Melbourne warehouses for eligible postcodes. Just choose "Pickup" at checkout.

Which mattress fits a top bunk?+

Top bunks need a thinner mattress so the guard rail stays effective. Each bunk lists the maximum thickness for every position, and the product page only shows mattresses that fit.

What does pre-order mean?+

The item is on its way to our warehouse. You can order now at today's price, and the estimated arrival date is shown on the product, in your cart, at checkout and in your confirmation email.

Do you offer Afterpay and Zip?+

Yes. Pay in 4 interest-free instalments with Afterpay, or use Zip. You can also pay with PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay or card.
