How is freight calculated?+

At checkout we send your postcode and each item's weight and size to the carrier for a live quote. If your order ships from two or three warehouses, each one is quoted and the total is shown before you pay.

Can I pick up my order?+

Yes. Click & collect is free from our Sydney and Melbourne warehouses for eligible postcodes. Just choose "Click & collect" at checkout.

Which mattress fits a top bunk?+

Top bunks need a thinner mattress so the guard rail stays effective. Every bunk lists the maximum thickness for each position, and the product page only shows mattresses that fit.

What does pre-order mean?+

The item is on its way to our warehouse. Order now at today's price; the arrival date is shown on the product, in your cart, at checkout and in your confirmation email.

Do you offer Afterpay and Zip?+

Yes. Pay in 4 interest-free instalments with Afterpay, or use Zip. PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and all major cards are accepted too.
