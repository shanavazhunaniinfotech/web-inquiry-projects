How is freight calculated?+

At checkout we send your postcode and each item's weight and size to the carrier for a live quote. If your order ships from more than one warehouse, each is quoted and the total is shown before you pay.

Can I pick up my order?+

Yes. Click & collect is free from our Sydney and Melbourne warehouses for eligible postcodes.

Which mattress fits a top bunk?+

Every bunk lists the maximum thickness for each position, and the product page only shows mattresses that fit.

What does pre-order mean?+

The item is on its way to our warehouse. Order at today's price; the arrival date shows on the product, in your cart, at checkout and in your confirmation email.

Do you offer Afterpay and Zip?+

Yes, plus PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and all major cards.

What warranty do I get?+

Every bed carries the manufacturer's warranty: up to 5 years on bunk frames. Claims are handled by our team.
