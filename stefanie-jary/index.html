Step 3

Our honest recommendation

Best for most community centres

Choose Squarespace if…

  • Volunteers change often and need it to be simple
  • Nobody wants to manage plugin updates or security
  • Your events and bookings are fairly straightforward
  • You want one predictable monthly bill

Stay on WordPress if…

  • You run many recurring classes with complex filters
  • Room hire needs approvals, deposits or invoices
  • Someone (us or a volunteer) can keep it updated
  • You'd rather not move content right now

Either way, the new site will be:

Fresher, welcoming and easier for volunteers. It will have a clear "What's On", simple room hire, volunteering and donations, accessibility, and a short training session plus a how-to guide for your team.
