01 · Why stay on WordPress

Six reasons a refresh beats a rebuild.

01

No migration, no downtime

Every page, image and form stays put. We redesign on top of what works instead of rebuilding from zero.

02

Keep your Google rankings

Same web addresses, same history. Switching platforms often changes URLs and can drop you down search results for months.

03

Built for events & room hire

WordPress has the strongest calendars and booking tools: recurring classes, RSVPs, room approvals, deposits and invoices.

04

You own everything

The site, content and data belong to the centre. No locked-in platform, no price rises you can't leave.

05

Easy for volunteers

With locked Elementor templates and a simplified dashboard, volunteers only see what they need: events, news and photos.

06

Lower total cost

No rebuild, no re-entering content, no per-transaction platform fees on donations. Budget goes into design, not moving house.

Squarespace is a good tool for small brochure sites. For a busy community centre with classes, room hire and an existing WordPress site, refreshing WordPress is the stronger, cheaper choice.

03 · The usual worry

"But WordPress is hard for volunteers."

Not the way we set it up. Volunteers get a clean dashboard with three big buttons, and the design can't be broken by accident.

  • Locked page designs: edit text and photos, never break the layout
  • Automatic updates, daily backups and a firewall: security handled for you
  • Short training + video how-tos: for every common task
