    07 Support

    Questions, answered.

    Who can purchase from Aus OG Peps?

    We supply qualified professionals and research facilities aged 18 or over. All products are sold strictly for in vitro laboratory research and are not for human or veterinary use.

    How fast is shipping?

    Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days by Australia Post Express Post. Delivery is next business day within the Express Post network, or 2–3 business days outside it. Orders over $150 ship free.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay. You'll get an order confirmation email as soon as payment goes through.

    Can I see the COA before I buy?

    Yes. Every product has a published certificate of analysis with a verification code. Try the COA check above.

    Is the packaging discreet?

    Every order ships in plain, unmarked packaging, with no reference to the contents or our brand on the outside.

    Can I return my order?

    Because these are research-grade materials, we can't accept returns once dispatched. If anything arrives incorrect or damaged, contact us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll arrange a replacement or refund.
